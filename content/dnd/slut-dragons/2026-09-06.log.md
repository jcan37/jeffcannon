## Scene 1: Getting the party back up

**Ruling: stabilizing.** Anyone can stabilize a downed creature with a Medicine check, DC 10. Success brings them to **1 HP**.
**Ruling: spells per turn.** You can cast a cantrip plus a leveled spell in one turn, but not two leveled spells (e.g. Healing Word and Goodberry together).

- **Razageth** stayed back and hit the jelly with sorcerous burst for 6 cold. The plan: Razageth gets Aracheus up, Aracheus heals the rest.
- **Tanner** made a death save on a 20 (success). Penny's roll was 19. Penny has no HP; she counts as a magic item/weapon.
- **Jelly** hit Strider (15 to hit vs. AC 15) for 12 (Jeff corrected it down from 13).
- **Strider** moved next to Aracheus and cast **Cure Wounds** for 14. He also moved Hunter's Mark onto the jelly. Ruling: Jeff confirmed the mark only transfers after the marked target dies. Brendan described his Hunter's Mark transfer as a Tai Chi hand motion; the others thought spitting on the target suited his character better.
- **Strider's outsider name.** Jeff asked Brendan for a cover name, since "Mike Boland" is his character's true name. The table settled on **"Strider"** for outsiders. "Mike Boland" stays inside the party. Jeff renamed the token "Strider."
- **Aracheus** got up and cast **Healing Word** on **Varasst** (2d4+2). He swung his quarterstaff without shillelagh (+3, d8+1), hit on an 8, and dealt 7. Varasst used his **Rejuvenate** bite (2d8 + Wis) to heal Strider for 11.
- **Razageth** stabilized **Tanner** with Medicine, bringing him to 1 HP.
- **Tanner** used reckless attack (16) for 13 damage, and the jelly **split** as a reaction. Jeff explained reactions: opportunity attacks, Shield, and the like.
- One jelly knocked **Tanner out again**. The other's 13 missed Strider.
- **Strider** tried **Fire Bolt** ("Brendan has powers"). Ruling: a ranged spell attack in melee is at disadvantage. He switched to his short sword and missed with a 1.
- **Aracheus** cast **Shillelagh** and hit for 8. He is down to one spell slot. Varasst moved behind Tanner and used Rejuvenate on him: 2d8 + Wis = **17 HP**.
- **Razageth** used his **last spell slot** on **Chromatic Orb (ice)**. It hit, killed the first jelly, rolled doubles, bounced to the second, and **froze and killed it** too. Mike: *"I did a fucking double ice orb… Everyone was fucking pissed they weren't me."*
- Weaknesses the table noted for these jellies: swords, psychic, ice.

## Scene 2: The statue room is empty
Jeff confirmed the explosion room held **no treasure**: *"You did all that work… to just get blown up."* He pointed them back toward the main hall for the real treasure.

## Scene 3: The orc reinforcements
- **Tanner** announced he was done with the dungeon and headed for the exit alone. He ran into orcs at the cave door, and everyone rolled initiative.
- **Reveal:** Jeff explained the "something" from the earlier long rest. An **orc scout** came in while the party slept, saw its dead war party, and left to fetch more. It had **nothing to do with the dwarves** the party murdered. It came back with **three orcs**.
- Ruling: orcs can bonus-action dash (their Aggressive trait).
- **Orc 1** hit Tanner (23) for 6.
- Strider could only dash toward Aracheus.
- **Tanner** raged and used reckless attack for 14. Ruling: rage damage bonus is +2 at this level.
- **Orc 2** hit Tanner for 7, halved to 3 by rage.
- **Razageth**'s sorcerous burst missed. Jeff gave the orcs three-quarters cover (+5 AC) from his angle.
- **Orc 3** hit Tanner for 7, halved. Tanner was holding all three orcs at the cave mouth on about 1 HP.
- **Aracheus** got Tanner's consent and fired his **acid breath** as a 30-ft line (DC 13 Dex) through Tanner. The orc saved and took 5. Tanner was "melted" too, and Aracheus then healed him with **Healing Word** for 7. *"I melted you a little bit, but I brought you right back."* Ruling: Healing Word is a bonus action with 60 ft range. Breath options were a 15-ft cone or a 30-ft line.
- **Penny** rolled a 2 on the d6: *"Enemies hold back against you because you're carrying a baby."* Creatures have **disadvantage on attacks against Tanner for one minute**. Cam explained Penny for the table: each roll lowers the d20 threshold until it triggers, then a d6 picks the effect, and the 6 is the flamethrower.
- **Strider** cast Fire Bolt for **14 fire** and the doorway orc started burning.
- **Tanner** killed it, *chopping out its legs so it fell throat-first onto his planted greatsword.* Penny rolled 14 and the threshold went back to 19.
- An orc hit Tanner for 12. There was an argument over whether his rage had dropped when he went unconscious. Jeff let him keep it, so 6 damage.
- Razageth dashed up. **Ruling:** a dead body is difficult terrain (double movement) and you can stop on one.
- **Aracheus** stood on a corpse and missed with shillelagh (10). **Varasst** jumped off his shoulder (spending half his movement) and landed a poison bite (15): 1d4 + Wis = 4 piercing, and the target is **poisoned** until Varasst's next turn.
- **Strider** finally learned the Roll20 hand tool and shot an arrow (20 to hit) for 8.
- **Tanner** finished the last orc for 7. Jeff: *"You guys really fucking walled these orcs off. Kinda sweet."*

## Scene 5: The woman in the bushes, Halia Thornton
- Jeff rolled a d20 on departure ("if it lands on seven, something is going to happen"). The party travelled a few hours to the outskirts of **"Fandolin"** *(likely Phandalin)*.
- A woman's voice came from the bushes: *"I almost didn't recognize you guys in those outfits. I would not recommend going back to town right now."*
- **Tanner** spun and threw his trident at her. **Aracheus** stepped in the way (rolled 11 against a DC of 10) and took the hit: 1d8+3 = 4. Aracheus passed the topple save (15 vs. DC 13).
- She asked if they had forgotten her: *"I put you in that prison and I left that door unlocked for a reason."* Aracheus recovered: *"Of course I did not forget you."*
- **What she told them:**
  - *"I'm not a big fan of Harvin either."*
  - The party is **wanted in town**. The **bounty is 500 gp** for whoever burned Harbin's house, **posted by Harbin himself**.
  - She recommended they see **Adabra Gwynn** ("Dauber Gwyn," "Darbragwyn") first for **healing potions**. Adabra is at **"Umbridge Hill"** *(as heard; likely Umbrage Hill)*, south of town near the route they took to the dwarven excavation. Jeff showed it on the Sword Coast map (blue = Phandalin, orange = her hill, purple = where they'd come from).
  - She suggested they could **"work together to replace the mayor."**
  - She gave her name: **Halia Thornton** (heard "Isoliah," later "Hollya").
- Tanner mouthed off: *"That next trident will find you."* She replied: *"Why is this really short gnome talking to me like this? Does he know that I can slit his throat if I wanted to?"* Tanner charged with his greatsword. The others restrained him and initiative was called but not resolved.
- **Tanner announced he would turn himself in.** The plan: have the reward sent to "Mike Boland," then break out. He gave farewells: a chest-bump for Razageth, *"we've had our differences"* to Aracheus, a pat for Varasst, and *"Mike Boland, I know your true name, and I still love you"* to Strider.
- Halia blocked him: *"Do you intend to tell anyone in town that I'm helping you?"* He promised not to. She offered to **disguise him** to get through town. He declined, and she **vanished into the shadows**. She left **one ration each** for the three who stayed.

## Scene 6: Tanner in Phandalin
- He walked in openly, wearing the blood-covered dwarf clothes, with a little helmet on Penny. Townsfolk shut their windows.
- At the **Miners' Exchange** he asked about *"the reward for that gang of miscreants."* **Deception** roll: 11, with evil points adjusting it to a **15** total (Jeff first said −2, then said the mechanic actually adds). The miners were confused but didn't recognize him.
- *"Why don't you just show me?"* *"With pleasure."* He ripped off the dwarf outfit: *"Gentlemen, take me away."*
- He told them to send the gold to **Mike Boland**, *"or he'll come and kill all of you,"* and to leave it by the remains of Harbin's house. The miner asked: *"Who's Mike Boland?"* Then Tanner learned **the 500 gp comes from Harvin** and realized **Harbin is alive**.
- He **broke his restraints** (17) and ran north toward Harbin's burned house, calling the others on the **Sending Stone** (it went to voicemail first).
- Outside the **town marshal's hall** a big guard shouted *"There he is! Get him!"* Jeff showed AI art of the guard, which the table read as a paladin with a spear.
- On the Sending Stone: *"He's alive. Harbin's fucking alive,"* then he hung up.
- Back with the others, Strider: *"Harbin still draws breath."*
- Tanner dodged through alleys past the burned house and **dove into a haystack at Alderleaf Farm**. Stealth at disadvantage. Jeff set the DC at 15, then lowered it to 10. He rolled 18 and 20 and is **hidden**. The town is still searching ("Assassin's Creed" heightened awareness), but he has lost his pursuers for now.
