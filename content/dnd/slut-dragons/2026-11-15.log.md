## Recap
The last session ended with Cryovain landing in front of the party outside the burning Townmaster's Hall, demanding their gold, while Aracheus lay unconscious inside. This session, Razageth Commanded the dragon away, the party hid and had downtime in the Miners' Exchange, looted a ruined Phandalin, and got cold-resistance ingredients from Adabra at Umbrage Hill. Then they followed Gorrin's call to Axeholm, where Aracheus put Cryovain to sleep at the gate.

Mechanic: he draws three, Jeff tells him the faces, and he picks one without knowing the outcome. Razageth is waiting for a desperate situation. Aracheus: *"eyeing the cards like Gollum with the ring."*
  - **Crafting:** Jeff says Aracheus's herbalism kit lets him craft potions if he buys materials somewhere. Higher rarities take longer. Aracheus read that he can make 25 gp of value per day, which covers a Potion of Healing, healer's kit, candle or antitoxin. Nothing can be made now without materials.
  - **The vault:** Strider: it's a magic vault, so lockpicking won't work, and it electrocutes you. Razageth's Arcana was 7. Aracheus's Arcana is +0 (Jeff: druid magic is Wisdom, Arcana is Intelligence) and he rolled 10. Neither figured out how to disable it. It's stone with no levers or dials, and Tanner already tried the handle on an earlier visit. It's a vault room, not a box. Aracheus turned into a giant badger (Varasst had never seen that form and danced around) and dug down 5 ft, over 10 ft, and up. That took the full hour, and he hit a **magical barrier** at the floor of the vault room. Jeff nearly gave a hint and then didn't: *"because you can't roll high enough."*

## Scene 5: Phandalin in ruins (0:42–1:09)
- Outside at sunset: the **Townmaster's Hall is rubble** and Phandalin looks destroyed. Parts are ice, parts are burned, and there are remnants of people the dragon scooped up and ate. The streets are empty, though people may be hiding in buildings. Jeff: the dragon *"doesn't care that much about you guys... he's not gonna wait around."*

He kicked in the shed door with a lumberjack roar and found the pitchfork they'd pinned him with and his little shirt hanging there, but no Alderleaf. They had left him there about a day ago. The house looked the same as before. Strider stashed the shirt. *"That bitch from the miner's place. She fucking helped him."* Scene: *"I will find you, Dragon Bait."*

Results: **one ration each (4)**, plus **a flask and a vial** for Razageth. No poison (100 gp). The spyglass (1,000 gp) and a wizard spellbook were off-limits or useless. Jeff: in Phandalin, **only Adabra sells Potions of Healing**.

Razageth does not regain sorcery points on a short rest at level 4 (Sorcerous Restoration starts at level 5). He also suggested multiple short rests in downtime.

## Scene 6: Umbrage Hill (1:09–1:47)
- About an hour's travel. Adabra lives in the tower on top of the hill. **Groups of refugees from Phandalin** are huddled outside, at least a dozen, with more inside. Jeff: the townsfolk are extremely weak.
- Plan: Strider goes in first, because Adabra doesn't know him but the refugees might. His goal: *"acquire frost potions, or at least inquire as to how one could acquire them."*
- Strider rock-phoned Gorrin from outside: *"Where are you guys? We've been waiting."* Strider: *"Calm your tits, Rocky."*
- Strider found the **Cloak of Billowing** in his inventory (bonus action: it billows dramatically for 1 minute). Jeff had added it before they started playing: *"I am so happy that you finally noticed your cloak of billowing."* He dashed 60 ft around the crowd and hopped the fence (difficult terrain). The tower has only one door.
- He cast **Thaumaturgy** to fake a dragon's roar from a point up in the sky. *"To your surprise, you hear another roar... it is actually the dragon that's flying over."* The refugees cowered, which gave him **advantage on Stealth** (17, +6), and he passed unnoticed with his cloak "moderately billowing" (*"for the sake of the AI"*).
- Inside, more refugees. Adabra was cooking at a stove pot for them. Strider put **10 gp on her stove**: *"I would have words... Not here, somewhere private."* She was half offended and said they could talk upstairs in a minute.
- His Stealth check while waiting was 12. A refugee: *"That's him! He's one of the ones who burned Phandalin."* Adabra: *"What do you mean he burned Phandalin?"* Strider grabbed her wrist (*"Quickly, come!"*), and she wouldn't go willingly. He pulled out the baby shirt: *"Please, my son, you have to help me."* Then: *"You misunderstand. Five minutes of your time and I'll be gone forever."* Adabra: *"I think you should go now. You're not welcome here."* He lost the 10 gp.
- On the way out he **stabbed a refugee, who dropped**, earning **his third evil point**. Strider now has 3 evil points; every other PC has 2.
- **Evil point ruling:** each one subtracts from every Charisma roll where you're trying to persuade someone. Killing innocents adds them, and saving innocents removes them. Strider's evil point from stabbing the refugee stands even though Adabra revived him.
- As Strider sprinted away, **Adabra spoke some words, healed the stabbed refugee and brought him back to life.** (At the table someone said Strider tossed a Goodberry on the body. That was likely a joke.)
- Perception: Aracheus got 16, and Jeff whispered him the result in chat. Aracheus told the others that the **dragon's roar came from the south side of Umbrage Hill**, toward Axeholm. Jeff rolled three 1s in a row (*"something to be happening"*).
- The table joked about wiping out the refugees and "flipping our character arc."
- Strider rang Gorrin: *"Thought you said the coast was clear."* Gorrin: *"It's a little scary over here... now it's getting dark out. I'm not sure I want to be here much longer. But we kind of need you down here."*

## Scene 8: Axeholm (1:57–2:21)
- It's about two hours south and fully dark (everyone has darkvision). Tanner's token was removed. Jeff: *"He's technically with you... next time he'll show up wherever you guys are."*

A dark, fifteen-foot-wide passageway, flanked by forty-foot-high bulwarks of smooth, seamless stone. An iron portcullis, covered with rust, blocks the entrance. Arrow slits carved into the bulwarks are dark, revealing no hint of occupation."* It smells like sulfur.

- Strider found **a hole bashed through the center of the portcullis** and squeezed through (Jeff joked that the cloak snagged). Inside is a hall with more arrow slits, leading to double doors.
- Varasst climbed the wall into an arrow slit and crawled along the ceiling. Jeff corrected himself: **Varasst's room holds the winch** and the gate lever, plus about seven crossbows. Strider can't open the gate from where he is.
- Razageth's Insight was 12. From the hole's outline, **Gorrin made it**.

## Scene 9: Cryovain at the gate (2:21–2:48)
- *"The cryovain is back, and he swoops down, and he is ready to attack you."* Initiative: Strider first, then Aracheus 19 and Razageth 15. Matt Boland is with Strider.
- **Strider:** he rock-phoned Gorrin, and it rang and rang with no answer. Voicemail: *"I'm coming to find you to fucking kill you."* He ran through the double doors into the **great hall**: a 40-ft ceiling on four stone pillars, dust on the flagstones, arrow slits high in the north and south walls, and a stone balcony 20 ft up over the east exit. Every spot is in crossbow range. He fired an arrow out through the hole at the dragon and missed. Ruling: there's no poison for arrows.
- **Aracheus:** Cryovain was right next to him. He used **Misty Step** (bonus action, no opportunity attack) to get through the portcullis, ran toward the double doors, and cast **Sleep** (60 ft) through the hole. Wisdom save DC 13: **Cryovain failed** and was incapacitated until the end of his next turn. Varasst relayed the crossbow room and lever telepathically.
- **Razageth:** Mage Hand can't aim the crossbows, so he readied an action to have it fire a bolt through the slit if the dragon comes through. He moved through the portcullis, staying within 30 ft. Cryovain was incapacitated on his turn and looked about to pass out.
- **Strider:** *"This room is giving me the creeps."* He kicked the **east doors** off their hinges. The east hall is in ruins after an earthquake collapsed the ceiling. It's difficult terrain, and *"the stench of death hangs heavy here."* He headed down a **long tunnel/passage** with Matt Boland.
- **Aracheus** followed Strider (he didn't attack, so Cryovain stays asleep). Varasst dashed to the south end.
- **Razageth** let the Mage Hand go and dashed deeper inside.
- **Cryovain failed his second save and is asleep** (Jeff put an X on him). Sleep lasts up to a minute, so 10 rounds as long as no one attacks him. Jeff: *"This is the best I've ever seen it used... you literally just put a dragon that would have fucked you guys up to sleep."*
- **End:** the dragon stays asleep for about eight more rounds. The party is inside Axeholm. They know Gorrin came here with a bunch of people and bashed through the front, but there's no other sign of him, and he isn't answering.

## Rulings summary
- Starting heal: 9 HP each, with any excess as temp HP.
- A natural 20 on initiative means going first, not a crit.
- Cryovain is too big to follow them into a basement or burrow.
- Deck mechanic: draw three, Jeff names the faces, pick one blind.
- Arcana is Intelligence. Druid casting is Wisdom.
- Herbalism kit crafting needs bought materials. Time scales with rarity.
- Armory: each PC will choose one weapon next session, no expensive armor. Provisions: items under 50 gp, up to 50 lb.
- Short rest: hit dice up to your level, rolled one at a time. Sorcery points do not come back at level 4 (Sorcerous Restoration starts at level 5).
- Evil points: minus to persuasion Charisma rolls. Gained for killing or harming innocents, lost for saving them. The evil point stands even if the victim is revived.
- Potion of Cold Resistance: halves cold damage. 100 gp of ingredients and 5 days of brewing, faster with helpers. Can't brew while walking.
- Fortress crossbows don't swivel. Mage Hand can fire one with a readied action.
- Misty Step avoids opportunity attacks. Sleep: incapacitated, then unconscious on a second failed save, for up to 1 minute unless attacked.
