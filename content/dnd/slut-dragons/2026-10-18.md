## Recap

**Before the fight:** Nobody had long rested. Razageth had one level-2 slot and his sorcery points, and Aracheus had no spell slots. Jeff gave everyone **16 temporary HP** (he was late to the game). Strider had his dog, Matt Boland, back. In the six seconds before the fight, Razageth turned 3 sorcery points into a level-2 slot. Jeff vetoed dragging Alderleaf out of the shed as a human shield.

**Strider's dad:** The three-armed guard went first. He is **Mark Boland, Strider's father**. *"As if you haven't already made me embarrassed enough with your actions,"* he said, and he conjured a **shadow blade** in his third arm. He fired three crossbow bolts, at Matt Boland, Tanner and Aracheus. All three missed, and he slipped into the shadows behind the hall. Strider cast Thaumaturgy and boomed *"Go ahead and hide, you fucking coward!"*

**The Sun God:** The gladiator at the door lit up. His eyes turned to light, light cracked through his skin, and a radiant aura burned anyone who stood close to him. His spear hits did extra radiant damage. The whole party hit him. Penny started crying, which did psychic damage to anyone who ended a turn next to her.

**Family business:** Mark stepped out of the shadows and put a poisoned bolt in Tanner for 30 damage. He came face to face with Strider: *"What the fuck did you say to me before?"* Strider shouted a name back at him (unclear on the tape), and Mark hissed *"Be quiet! Don't say that name."* Jeff gave Strider Heroic Inspiration for standing up to his dad, and Strider stabbed him for 13. *"My own son. How could you?"* Razageth upcast **Command: "Halt"** on both enemies, and both failed. Mark shed a single tear. On his next turn Strider aimed an arrow at his father's face, said *"Run!"*, and shot the Sun God instead.

**It falls apart:** The Sun God knocked Aracheus prone and dropped him. Mark declared *"I'm gonna kill all your friends and then I'm gonna deal with you"* and cut Razageth down with three shadow-blade strikes. Strider used Cure Wounds on Aracheus, revived Matt Boland, and the dog charged the Sun God for 13. Tanner crit the Sun God for 18 and later knocked Mark prone with a thrown trident. The Sun God healed himself and pulled back, still bloodied. Mark dropped Aracheus again, killed Matt Boland again, and stabbed Strider (*"I can't let you terrorize this city anymore"*). His thrown shadow blade crit Tanner for 49.

**The last one standing:** Strider said *"Forgive me, father,"* and dashed for the hall's front door. He threw himself into it and bounced off. Mark caught him with a critical hit and slammed him into the door. **Everyone is unconscious, not dead.** Varasst slipped away unnoticed and is hiding in the bushes outside the Townmaster's Hall, watching over the bodies. The Sun God ran north. The table chose not to find out where they wake up.
