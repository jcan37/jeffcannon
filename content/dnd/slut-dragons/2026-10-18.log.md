## Rulings summary
- Six-second prep turn when initiative is already rolled. 16 temp HP each (Jeff was late). Temp HP goes first, and a late fix for an unapplied pool was allowed.
- A tied-up NPC doesn't count as a shield.
- Attack bonus = proficiency + ability. D&D Beyond does the math.
- Rage: resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing, and casting a spell ends it. Reckless Attack covers Strength attacks only. Jeff also halved Penny's psychic damage for a raging Tanner.
- Penny d6, as read: 5 = hungry crying (1d4 psychic to anyone ending a turn within 5 ft, 10 rounds or until fed). 6 = fire (up to three enemies within 30 ft, 1d10 each).
- Upcast Command can target two creatures. "Halt" = no movement, attacks, bonus actions or opportunity attacks on their next turn.
- Opportunity attacks trigger on leaving reach, not entering it. Disengage costs a main action, and rogue-types can do it as a bonus action.
- Primal Companion revive is a Magic action and Cure Wounds is an action. Jeff allowed both in one turn "just this time." Healing Word is a bonus action.
- Prone: standing costs half your movement, melee attacks against a prone creature have advantage, and it stands on its own turn. Crits double the dice. Thrown weapons have to be picked back up.
- No weaknesses in 5e, only resistances. Invisibility ends when you attack. You can't drink a potion while unconscious, but Varasst can give one.
- Losing this fight means knocked out, not killed, and there's a long rest while unconscious.
