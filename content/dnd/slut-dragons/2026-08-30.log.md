- **Orc javelins Razageth.** The orc said *"No forgiveness, I am going to rape you now,"* dashed up, and threw a javelin at Razageth: 15 to hit, 7 damage. Razageth stayed up.

Tanner strolled in over the outcrop with *"Someone order a trident? Sandwich, extra mustard."* The rest of the party pointed out they had all just risked their lives coming back to save him.

## Scene 2: New rest rules, loot, and talking to the dwarves

**Rulings Jeff introduced:**
- **Rations.** A long rest costs one ration. A long rest without one gives a level of exhaustion. The six levels: disadvantage on ability checks → speed halved → disadvantage on attacks → … → death at 6. The dwarves (named **Dazlin** and **Norvis**) handed out two rations per PC.
- **One long rest per 24 hours.** Only about an hour of the day had passed, so the party had roughly 15 hours of downtime. Jeff noted they could use downtime for things like scribing spell scrolls, and that he would roll for random events during it.
- A short rest lets you spend hit dice but generally doesn't restore spell slots.

**Loot from the orcs** (the jellies had nothing):
- 4 greataxes, 4 javelins, 4 sets of hide armor
- **40 gp**, 10 per orc, which Jeff added "because you guys are so keen to get some gold." It was meant to be split evenly. Someone said "I'm holding the gold" *(the holder is uncertain; possibly Tanner)*.
- Someone suggested scooping ochre jelly into "that jar" (the old oil jar/glass) and someone said they put jelly in it *(who holds the jelly jar is uncertain; possibly Razageth)*.

**The dwarves:**
- Jeff noted they were terrified and were realizing the party had no intention of sharing treasure.
- Tanner admitted he had spat in their faces earlier. He then said *"Look, I've got a baby,"* and persuaded them to keep watch while the party rested. They agreed.
- Razageth and Aracheus questioned them. Jeff did Gimli/Irish-style voices for them:
  - The site is **an old temple to "Amador" (as heard), the dwarf god of greed.** The dwarves are miners and have mining tools.
  - *"We know where all the secret tunnels are."* They offered to guide the party from the back but said *"we're no fighters."* They were also expecting a share of any treasure.
- Someone talked about pickpocketing their rations. Jeff said there was no need, they were going to hand them over anyway.

**The rest:** Jeff rolled through the hours: 5 hours, then 8 more, then sleep. As far as the party could tell, **nothing happened**. Tanner "posted up" in a back corner on 1 HP. Razageth bedded down behind a wall saying *"I don't trust these halflings."*

## Scene 4: Searching for secret doors

With no guides, Jeff had them search the walls square by square. *"Just to teach you a fucking lesson."*
- North room: just the dead jelly. **Tanner** spotted a secret door, but it only led back to a room they'd already seen.
- **Partially collapsed room.** It looked earthquake-damaged. Aracheus's perception (16) showed the rubble used to connect to another room. Ruling: his giant badger wild shape can burrow through dirt, mud, and ice but **not stone rubble**. Varasst is small enough to squeeze through but would be on his own for a long time. They didn't send him.
- **Behind the altar.** Multiple rolls found nothing (Razageth rolled a 1). Eventually **Tanner rolled a 20** and found a secret passage. *"We didn't need those fucking doors. We make our own path."*
- **Mining hallway.** It's a half-dug passage with a door into a **priests' bedroom**: three stone bed frames built into the floor. Lying on them did nothing.
- **Aracheus found another secret door** on a total of 10, after Jeff admitted he was making it easier. Behind it was a room with an empty stone (holy water) font and a stone wardrobe holding rotted remains of two suits of red leather armor. Aracheus bit the armor and passed a Con save. *"That's armor."*
- **Aracheus rolled a 20** on another wall and found another secret door.

## Scene 6: Second ochre jelly fight

Initiative: Aracheus 20, Strider 19, Tanner 14, Razageth 11, then the jelly.
- An ochre jelly hiding in the walls of the tunnel. Aracheus hit with his shillelagh staff (d8+2) and stepped back to avoid an opportunity attack.
- Strider cast Hunter's Mark and hit for 8. Jeff joked about whether he hated being a ranger and later suggested the **Arcane Archer** subclass, which can curve arrows.
- Ruling: Speak with Animals doesn't work on an ooze.
- The jelly swung at Aracheus with a 6 and missed.
- **Tanner** went into reckless attack (advantage for him, and enemies get advantage on him). He hit for 13, and the jelly **split in two**. He also used **Rage** (two per day).
- **Razageth** used bonus-action innate sorcery plus **Chromatic Orb** (3d8, ice). He spent a sorcery point to reroll low dice and dealt 15, killing one half.
- **Heroic inspiration.** Tanner reminded everyone he had sung his **Encouraging Song** after the long rest (musician feature), giving heroic inspiration to **Strider** and **Aracheus**.
- Aracheus hit the other half for 6. Strider couldn't see it from the doorway, so Jeff moved him up. Strider killed it with his short sword (d6+3, plus a Hunter's Mark d6).
- **Surprise:** a third ochre jelly, there the whole time, dropped from the ceiling on Razageth: 18 to hit, 6 bludgeoning + 6 acid. Razageth fell to **4 HP**.
- Tanner (raging) hit it for 11 and it split. Razageth hit it with Shocking Grasp and it split again, then he retreated 30 ft to the hallway.
- Aracheus hit for 5 (no split). Strider moved Hunter's Mark and killed one jelly for 10.
- Jellies hit Tanner for 7 (halved to 3 by rage resistance). A swing at 13 missed.
- Tanner hit with reckless attack for 7 and it split again ("How many are we looking at? Four, five? Now we're at three!"). **Penny** rolled 19, then a 1 on the d6: **"Fire Giant Anger Strike,"** d10 fire to each of up to three creatures within 30 ft. She dealt 10, 4, and 1. All the jellies were bloodied but alive.
- Razageth cast **False Life** (2d4+4) and came back in.
- Aracheus missed on a 7 and was hit on the way out for 8 bludgeoning + 2 acid (acid resistance as an acid dragonborn).
- Strider fired an arrow through the line of jellies. Jeff ruled that anything under 10 total would hit an ally. He rolled 12 and killed one. Jeff allowed a curved-arrow attempt only on a nat 20. He rolled 11 and declined to use inspiration.
- One jelly **dashed away and disappeared**. Tanner called for an opportunity attack but didn't get one. The other jelly hit Tanner on a 23 (about 6 after resistance).
- Tanner hit on double sixes for 15. Jeff called for an insight/perception check (9, perceived nothing). Razageth's sorcerous burst (ice) did 6, and Aracheus finished the jelly with his staff for 7.

## Scene 7: Mining through to the statue chamber, and the gem explodes

- One of the PCs found another secret door. Jeff said "eight" might have been an exaggeration but there is probably one more secret spot. Aracheus noticed loose rubble on the unexplored part of the hallway.
- **Ruling:** using the dead dwarves' mining tools, getting through takes 40 person-hours, so about **10 hours** for the four of them. It's downtime work that can't happen while asleep. They broke through late at night.
- **The chamber:** the rubble of a shattered statue to the south. In a north alcove is a statue of a **horned dwarf staring greedily at a glowing green gem** in its hands.
- **Tanner** climbed the statue and pried the gem out. It glowed, crumbled to dust in his hands, and the statue lit up and **exploded**.
- Tanner and Aracheus made Dex saves. Tanner had advantage from Danger Sense, and one of them spent heroic inspiration *(which one is uncertain)*. Both rolled a 20 total, which still meant **19 piercing damage each** (half).
- Rage had worn off after the 8+ hours of digging. **Both Tanner and Aracheus dropped. Jeff ruled at the table: "you're dead."** Only Razageth and Strider were left standing.
- Initiative was rolled: Razageth first, Strider at 12. The ochre jelly that ran earlier, now **not bloodied** (recovered), moved in.
- Strider mentioned he had one slot left for Cure/Heal Wounds and wanted to use it on Aracheus so he could make Goodberries.
- **Session ended at 11 pm** at that moment. Tanner and Aracheus are down or dead, and Razageth and Strider face the jelly.

## Rulings summary
- Rations: 1 per long rest. Without one, gain a level of exhaustion (level 6 = death).
- One long rest per 24 hours. Jeff rolls for random events during downtime.
- A mastery longbow hit slows the target by 10 ft.
- Trident range 20/60 ft (long range at disadvantage).
- Hunter's Mark moves only after the marked target dies (bonus action).
- Shooting through a doorway gives half or three-quarters cover.
- Badger wild shape can't burrow through stone rubble.
- Speak with Animals doesn't work on oozes.
- Clearing rubble takes 40 person-hours with mining tools.
- **Evil points (new):** from now on, killing innocents earns evil points with unknown, bad effects. Saving people offsets them. **The party starts with 2** for the dwarves.
- **Level up** once the party gets out of this cave. Players can take levels in other classes. Cam is thinking about bard for *Disguise Self*.
