## Recap

**Back on their feet:** Jeff gave everyone 9 HP to start. If that went over max HP, the extra became temp HP. That brought **Aracheus** back to consciousness inside the burning hall. The fire had reached the first floor, and every turn inside meant fire damage. Cam wasn't at the table, so **Tanner dashed off to the tavern with Penny** and stayed out of the session.

**Command: flee:** Razageth rolled a natural 20 on initiative against Cryovain. With no slot left for Suggestion, he cast **Command** with a level-1 slot. Cryovain failed the save and flew about 160 ft straight up and away. Phandalin emptied out in panic toward a safehold the party doesn't know about. The party ran to the empty **Miners' Exchange** and hid there. On the way, Varasst's bite healed Aracheus for 6.

**Downtime at the Exchange:** Razageth studied the 13-card deck (Arcana 17 with advantage) and **attuned to it**. Drawing a card can have an extremely powerful effect, good or bad. Jeff's mechanic: Razageth draws three, Jeff tells him the card faces, and he picks one without knowing what it does. He's saving it for a desperate moment. Razageth (7) and Aracheus (10) both failed Arcana on the magic vault. Aracheus Wild Shaped into a **giant badger** and spent the full hour burrowing down, across and up into the vault. He hit a **magical barrier** at the floor. Jeff also said Aracheus's herbalism kit lets him craft potions if he buys the materials.

**Gorrin calls:** As they were about to long rest, the rock phone rang. It was **Gorrin**: he's at **Axholm**, the place to be if they want to escape the city, though it "might be a little haunted." Strider told the others "Wrong number," then called back. Gorrin said it was eerily quiet there and urged them to come now. They skipped the long rest.

**Phandalin in ruins:** At sunset the Townmaster's Hall was rubble and the town was mostly empty, part iced over, part burned, with signs of people the dragon had eaten.
- Strider went to save Alderleaf ("Dragon Bait"). The shed held only the pitchfork and Alderleaf's little shirt. Strider pocketed the shirt: *"I will find you, Dragon Bait."*
- At the armory, the shopkeeper (name not given on tape) told Aracheus, *"You've destroyed this town... take whatever you need and leave."* **Everyone gets a new weapon of their choice** (and armor if they lack it, nothing as expensive as half plate). When he asked about hidden stock, she said *"not that I would tell you, you lowly thief."* He left 10 gp on the counter.
- Razageth examined Cryovain's ice. It's the same kind of dragon magic as his and Aracheus's, only much stronger. Then he took his handful of ice to Barthen's Provisions (on tape "Barthun's"), which was deserted. Jeff's rule was items under 50 gp only: one ration each, plus a flask and a vial for Razageth. The poison was 100 gp, so no one has poison.
- The party short rested at sunset. Aracheus spent 2 hit dice and is at 26 HP.

**Umbrage Hill:** The refugees from Phandalin are camped at Adabra's tower. Strider went in alone because Adabra doesn't know him. He finally noticed his **Cloak of Billowing**, which Jeff had put in his inventory before the game. He cast **Thaumaturgy** to fake a dragon's roar overhead, and then **a real roar answered: Cryovain was flying over.** With advantage he slipped through the crowd (Stealth 17). He put 10 gp on Adabra's stove and said *"I would have words."* Then he failed a Stealth check (12), and a refugee shouted *"That's him! He's one of the ones who burned Phandalin."* Strider grabbed Adabra's wrist and showed her the baby shirt (*"Please, my son, you have to help me"*), but she told him: *"You're not welcome here."* He lost the 10 gp. On his way out he stabbed a refugee, who dropped, and that **earned him an evil point**. As he left, Adabra healed the man and brought him back.

**Evil points explained:** Evil points subtract from every Charisma roll where you're trying to persuade someone. Killing innocents earns them, and saving innocents removes them. Even knocking out a refugee for no reason earns one.

**Annatar's visit:** Aracheus (Perception 16) worked out that the dragon's roar came from the south, the direction of Axholm. Strider rang Gorrin, who said it was getting scary there, but *"we kind of need you down here."* Razageth used Alter Self to look like "sexy Sauron" and went in as **"Anaton"** (his Rings of Power persona, spelling uncertain), seeking a way to resist the dragon's frost. Adabra only has healing potions. She sold him the **ingredients for one Potion of Cold Resistance for 100 gp**. It halves cold damage and takes **five days** of crafting, less with help. Aracheus will brew it.

**Axholm:** About two hours south, in the dark, they reached a dwarven fortress at the base of a dark gray mountain that smells of sulfur. It has a 15-ft passage between 40-ft bulwarks, a rusted portcullis, and arrow slits holding **heavy crossbows loaded with five bolts each**. Something had **bashed a hole through the portcullis**, and Razageth's Insight said Gorrin made it. Strider squeezed through, and Varasst found the winch room with about seven crossbows and the gate lever. Then **Cryovain swooped back in.**
- Strider rang Gorrin. **No answer**, so he left a voicemail threatening to kill him. Strider ran into the great hall (40-ft ceiling, four pillars, a balcony 20 ft up over the east exit). His arrow at the dragon missed.
- Aracheus **Misty Stepped** through the portcullis and cast **Sleep** back through the hole. **Cryovain failed both saves and fell asleep.** He stays asleep for up to a minute unless someone attacks him.
- Razageth readied Mage Hand to fire a crossbow bolt if the dragon came through, then went inside.
- Strider kicked the east doors off their hinges. The east hall was wrecked by an earthquake and smelled of death. He headed down a long tunnel with Matt Boland, and Aracheus and Varasst followed.

The session ended with **Cryovain asleep outside for about eight more rounds**. The party is inside Axholm with no sign of Gorrin, though he clearly came here with a group of people and broke in.
