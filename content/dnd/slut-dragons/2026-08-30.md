## Recap

The party finished a brutal fight in the dwarf mine, taking down orcs and ochre jellies across multiple chambers. Aracheus was attacked by a jelly that had dropped from the ceiling in a secret room. Strider's hunter's mark and coordinated attacks brought down the remaining enemies. Tanner threw his trident over Strider's head to deliver a critical hit that ended the fight.

After the battle, two dwarf miners named Dazlin and Norvis provided rations and offered to guide the party through the temple's many secret doors. The site is an old temple to Amador, the dwarf god of greed. The party took a long rest while the dwarves kept watch. However, tensions rose after the rest when the dwarves were suddenly killed by the party. With no guides remaining, the party had to search for secret doors manually, finding passages behind the altar and discovering a priests' bedroom and other hidden chambers.

In one chamber, Tanner found the skeleton of a dwarf priest wearing rotted red leather armor and carrying a necklace with a tiny jeweled dagger inscribed with Dwarvish runes reading "Green is good." Multiple ochre jellies attacked during the exploration, with one memorable fight where Penny's Fire Giant Anger Strike dealt damage to several jellies at once. Varasst proved invaluable with his spider abilities.

The party spent hours mining through rubble to reach a final chamber. Inside stood a statue of a horned dwarf greedily holding a glowing green gem. When Tanner pried out the gem, it crumbled to dust and the statue exploded, dropping both Tanner and Aracheus. The session ended with Razageth and Strider facing a returning ochre jelly while their companions lay unconscious.
