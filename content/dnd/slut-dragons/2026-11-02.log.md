# Session log: Baby in the Chimney (2026-11-02)

- **Campaign:** Slut Dragons (Dragon of Icespire Peak). DM: Jeff. Played on Roll20.
- **Party:** Razageth (Mike), Tanner (Cam), Aracheus (Thomas), Strider (Brendan), plus Varasst and Matt Boland.
- **Source:** Plaud recording "D&D Slut Dragons 11/2" (~2h56m).
- **Spellings:** Names follow the glossary. On tape: Harbin is "Harvin/Arvin/Harmon," Halia is "Holly/Talia/Azelia," Phandalin is "Fandolin/Vandalin," and Strider's true name is "Mike Poland/Mike Bull."
- **Note:** The goliath who came up from the basement with the Sun God is called "the stone goliath/Stone God" on tape. He is presumably Gorrin, who was locked in the cell on 10/25. His name isn't said.

## Recap
The party killed Harbin, rescued Penny, hired the Sun God and the goliath, set Phandalin into panic, lost Halia, and ended face to face with Cryovain. Aracheus is still unconscious in the burning hall.

## Scene 1: Pregame and setup (0:00–0:22)
- Thomas swapped Entangle for **Create or Destroy Water** to fight the basement fire if needed.
- Cam's level-4 Tanner placeholders: **Tasha's Hideous Laughter**, Expertise in Intimidation and Perception, and Jack of All Trades.
- Fire clock: 3 rounds (18 seconds) had passed. Jeff: about 10 rounds per floor.
- Initiative was rerolled. Tanner went first, then Aracheus and Varasst, the Sun God, Razageth (16), Strider, and Matt Boland.

## Scene 2: Top floor, the Sun God flees (0:22–1:05)
- Tanner searched a side room (a single bed and an empty drawer) and raged.
- Aracheus hit the Sun God with **Flame Blade** (21 to hit, 9 fire) and a smite (2 necrotic). The Sun God failed his DC 13 Wisdom save and was **frightened**.
  - Ruling: the smite doesn't need concentration, so Flame Blade stays up. Jeff: *"Gemini is very stupid."*
  - Ruling: frightened gives disadvantage on attacks and ability checks, and the creature can't move closer.
- Varasst bit him. Jeff joked about the Sun God's AC.
- The Sun God made 3 spear attacks at disadvantage and hit Aracheus once for 7 piercing. He ran into the bathroom and locked it. The opportunity attacks missed.

## Scene 3: Looting Harbin's rooms (1:05–1:35)
- Razageth crit a Strength check and tore open the locked chest: **500 gp**, 125 each for the four PCs.
- The bedside drawer held **2 Potions of Healing and 1 Potion of Greater Healing**. Razageth announced only "two regular" ones and gave them to Strider and Tanner. He kept the Greater (*"I still have my greater healing"*).
- The Sun God passed his save and was no longer frightened.
- The desk: Strider's and Aracheus's Arcana checks (10) found only papers. Razageth, with advantage from his shard (*"Out of my way, fools!"*), found a **magical deck of 13 cards**. It needs attunement or a spell to identify. He pocketed it after a tickle fight with Tanner and Strider.
- Tanner found nothing under the bed. Varasst crawled under the bathroom door: window open, no one inside.
- Strider failed to pick the lock (7). Tanner failed to kick the door down. Aracheus picked it with thieves' tools (20). The Sun God had dropped out the window and took 2 falling damage.
- From the roof, Varasst (Perception 18) saw the **front door now open**.

## Scene 4: Harbin in the chimney (1:35–1:55)
- Razageth dashed downstairs and met the **Sun God and the charred goliath** coming up from the basement. Razageth to the goliath: *"When did you get so hot?"*
- Varasst (Perception 24) heard a cough in the bedroom chimney. **Harbin**, blackened, was inside it holding **Penny** and his musket. Cam: *"I didn't think Jeff would put my baby in a chimney."*
- Tanner dashed to the chimney. Harbin: *"Don't come any closer. I'll hurt your baby."* Jeff joked that Harbin blasted off on rocket shoes, then took it back.
- The parley:
  - Harbin wanted them to stop terrorizing his town and leave Phandalin forever.
  - Tanner told him the "miner chick" was plotting against him. Harbin: *"I knew that bitch was trying to cross me."*
  - Harbin's final terms: put out the fire, leave the building, and he brings Penny out when it's safe.
  - Tanner: Penny is a fire goliath and will be fine. Jeff: she's half gnome too.
  - Tanner sat on the chest, crossed his arms and refused to leave without her.
- Harbin fired his musket and missed. The deal was off, and he started climbing the chimney.

## Scene 5: The stairs fight (1:55–2:23)
- Razageth: Innate Sorcery, then missed (11). He drank a Potion of Healing (+5). Ruling: drinking a potion is a bonus action under the 2024 rules.
- Strider missed with his bow and put Hunter's Mark on the Sun God. Matt Boland jumped down the stairs and hit the Sun God for 11.
- The goliath slashed Aracheus for 13, leaving him at 9. Aracheus cast **Spike Growth** around the two guards and drank a potion (+8).
- Varasst chased Harbin up the chimney and bit him: 2 piercing, **poisoned**.
- The Sun God shield-bashed Aracheus: Strength save 8, 8 bludgeoning, prone. Aracheus failed concentration (10), so Spike Growth dropped. A spear hit for 9 piercing, and **Aracheus fell unconscious**.
- The Sun God's bonus action lit his 10-ft glow. He speared Matt Boland for 12 radiant and ran up the stairs past Aracheus. All opportunity attacks missed, including Razageth's dagger (11). Ruling: Strider must use a short sword in melee.
- Razageth's **level-2 Chromatic Orb** did 24 thunder to the goliath, cut to 19 by his reaction. The bounce to the Sun God missed (+3 AC reaction). Razageth converted sorcery points into a level-1 slot (bonus action). That was his last level-2 slot.
- Strider's arrow hit the goliath for 10 (**bloodied**).
  - Ruling: the signet ring works like a smite and only applies to the attack that hits.
  - He then used it on the Sun God (*"My name is Mike Boland"*) for extra radiant. The Sun God was bloodied, not dead.
  - Matt Boland (natural 20) bit the Sun God for 9.

## Scene 6: Harbin dies (2:23–2:32)
- Tanner couldn't reach with his sword. He threw his **trident** with Reckless Attack. Jeff gave a 10% chance of hitting Penny. It hit (16, 1d8+5 = 12, with rage included).
- Harbin fell. Tanner made the Dex save (16, Danger Sense advantage). **Harbin landed on the trident and it went through his head.**
- Tanner: *"Where's your rocket shoes now?"* He took Penny back unharmed.
- He carried Harbin's head downstairs on the trident. The Sun God and the goliath disengaged: *"We're only here for money, and now that you've killed the person paying us, we have no reason to be here."*
- Harbin had offered them 100 gp each per day. The party hired them instead for **one quest, to kill the dragon, at 100 gp each**. The goliath handed over a **rock phone**: *"Call me when you want us to come."*
- Jeff: *"You guys glad you killed the mayor of the town now?"*

## Scene 7: Phandalin panics (2:32–2:40)
- The fire was reaching the first floor as the party left. Townsfolk recognized the wanted party leaving another burning building. Seeing Harbin's head set off panic, then looting.
- Razageth and Aracheus shouted that they had saved the town and that Harbin was bringing the dragon. No one listened.
- Tanner stuck the head on a stake outside the hall.
- Halia watched from a distance (*"What the fuck? This is not what we agreed to"*). At the Exchange she was gone, *"like a ghost."* Jeff: she was clear about being discreet, and *"there's a good chance you never see her again."*
- Strider suggested wearing Harbin's face to calm the town. The party talked about looting the armory and fetching Alderleaf.

## Scene 8: Cryovain (2:39–2:45)
- Jeff's encounter roll matched Phandalin. **Cryovain** swooped down in front of them, there for the gold he could smell.
- He hates the dragonborn even more (*"abominations"*). To Tanner: *"You look like a chicken nugget to me, and I want to eat you."* He doesn't know or care who Harbin is: *"Unless you got food or gold for me... let's just stop talking."* Tanner tried to climb onto him.
- The guards are nearby and can be called. The party talked about fighting versus fleeing versus handing over part of the gold ("we become the Harbins"). **The session ended there.**
- Cryovain's hoard, including Razageth's wrist guard and the party's gold, is back in his lair.
- Aracheus was never revived. He's still unconscious in the burning hall. Jeff: Varasst bites him awake next time and he crawls out.

## Rulings summary
- The smite doesn't need concentration, so Flame Blade stays up.
- Frightened: disadvantage on attacks and ability checks, and the creature can't approach.
- Drinking a potion is a bonus action (2024 rules).
- The signet ring only empowers the attack that hits.
- A trident throw up the chimney had a 10% chance to hit Penny. Rage was included in 1d8+5.
- Cryovain shows up on a d20 encounter roll when the party leaves or arrives somewhere.
