## Recap

**At the windmill:** Aracheus spent downtime brewing a Potion of Healing with his herbalism kit. He paid Adabra Gwynn 25 gp for materials and got about halfway done. Razageth tried to intimidate Adabra into a discount, and she threw them out and stopped selling them potions. Aracheus came back and apologized, so she sold them 10 rations at a markup (2 gp each).

**Cryovain:** On the way back to Phandalin, Cryovain the white dragon landed in front of them, hungry for gold and food. Talking and deception both failed. His breath weapon dropped Razageth and Aracheus; Varasst shrugged it off and ran toward town for help. Cryovain swallowed all of their gold. Aracheus woke on a death save, used Land's Aid to bring Razageth back up (still in the dragon's jaws), and Razageth cast Command: Flee. The dragon let go and flew off, but tore away Razageth's time-travel wrist guard. Cryovain still has it.

**In Phandalin:** Strider and Tanner picked up a pitchfork from a hayfield shed. They went to a farmhouse, where the halfling farmer Alderleaf recognized them from wanted posters. They gagged him, raided his pantry, and kidnapped him. A stone goliath veteran chased them down, and Strider's companion dog died holding him off.

**Reunion:** The party regrouped outside town. Tanner tricked Varasst into heading for Harbin's burned-down house, so everyone went back in after him. They tied Alderleaf up in the hayfield shed and found Varasst safe near the Miner's Exchange.

**Halia's deal:** Tanner shouted "intruder!" and drew out Halia Thornton. She won't be seen helping them, but she hid them in the cellar (unlocked this time) and offered a deal: take out Harbin Wester, she becomes townmaster, and their wanted status goes away. She also hinted that the vault opens with a spell, not a key. Aracheus and Razageth reached level 4.

**Townmaster's Hall:** Harbin has been living in the Townmaster's Hall since his house burned down. The party tried to Sleep the guard at the door. He resisted, and initiative was rolled. Then a second, hidden guard stepped out: three arms, very fast. Strider recognized him.
