# Slut Dragons: the story so far

## Where things stand
The party is back together in Phandalin at night, attacking the Townmaster's Hall where Harbin Wester has been living since they burned his house. Aracheus's Sleep failed on the door guard, and a second, hidden three-armed guard who looks like Strider has stepped out with initiative 25. Halia Thornton has promised to become mayor and clear their names if they take Harbin out. They are low on spells and gold, and Cryovain has Razageth's wrist guard.

## The party
- **Razageth** (Mike): dragonborn sorcerer, now level 4. Carries a shard (name uncertain) that gives him extra damage but speaks to him and hits back with psychic damage. Cryovain tore off his time-travel wrist guard. He has no gold left.
- **Tanner** (Cam): gnome barbarian, now multiclassed into bard, level 3. Carries baby Penny, whose random effects keep changing fights. Beheaded one of the dwarf guides, turned himself in for the bounty and then escaped. Wears splint armor he isn't proficient in and has a pitchfork as a spare "trident."
- **Aracheus** (Thomas): dragonborn druid with pet spider Varasst, now level 4 (+2 Wis, 31 max HP). Half-finished brewed Potion of Healing, one Potion of Healing, no gold.
- **Strider** (Brendan): ranger, level 3. His true name, Mike Boland, stays inside the party. Wears a cosmetic Cloak of Billowing heirloom and probably holds the "Green is good" necklace (uncertain). His dog companion, Matt Boland, died holding off a guard and returns on his next long rest.
- Every PC has 2 evil points, for killing the dwarves. Each one is −1 on Charisma checks except Intimidation, which they improve.

## Key people
- **Harbin Wester**: Townmaster of Phandalin. He survived his burning house, posted a 500 gp bounty on the party, and has a musket.
- **Halia Thornton**: runs the Miners' Exchange and wants Harbin gone. She nearly killed Strider and Tanner with a poisoned blade, but has hidden them twice and offered the mayoral deal.
- **Adabra Gwynn**: midwife at the Umbrage Hill windmill. Grateful at first, she threw the party out after Razageth threatened her and no longer sells them potions.
- **Cryovain**: the white dragon. He swallowed 119 gp of their gold and holds Razageth's wrist guard.
- **Alderleaf**: halfling town farmer and Harbin supporter, left tied up in his own shed.
- **The manticore**: driven from its lair by the dragon. It was paid 50 gp to leave.
- **Townmaster's Hall guards**: the big "Sun God" at the door, the hidden three-armed guard, and the "stone goliath" veteran who killed Strider's dog.

## Sessions
- **The Dwarf Temple (2026-08-30):** The party finished off orcs and ochre jellies in the temple of Amador (uncertain), dwarf god of greed, then killed their dwarf guides Dazlin and Norvis and had to find the secret doors themselves. Prying the green gem from a horned statue made it explode, dropping Tanner and Aracheus.
- **Green Gems and a Bounty (2026-09-06):** The party got back up, beat an orc counterattack and took 15 green gems. Halia warned them of Harbin's 500 gp bounty. Tanner turned himself in, learned Harbin was alive, broke free, and hid in a haystack at Alderleaf Farm.
- **The Manticore and the Vault (2026-09-24):** Tanner and Strider failed to crack the Exchange's electrified magic vault and attacked Halia, who nearly killed them both. Razageth and Aracheus fought a manticore at Adabra's windmill, ended up paying it 50 gp, and each got a Potion of Healing.
- **Cryovain Takes His Toll (2026-10-04):** Razageth got the pair thrown out of the windmill, and then Cryovain dropped them both, ate their gold and took the wrist guard. Tanner and Strider kidnapped Alderleaf. Once regrouped, the party took Halia's deal and moved on the Townmaster's Hall.
