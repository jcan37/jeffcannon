# Slut Dragons: the story so far

## Where things stand
Phandalin is in ruins. The Townmaster's Hall burned to rubble, the dragon iced and ate parts of the town, and the survivors fled to **Adabra's tower at Umbrage Hill**. Those refugees now know Strider as one of the people who burned the town. Gorrin called the party to **Axeholm**, a dwarven fortress about two hours south of Umbrage Hill. When they arrived, he didn't answer his rock phone, but there was a hole he had bashed through the portcullis. Then **Cryovain** swooped in. Aracheus cast **Sleep** on him, and the session ended with **the dragon asleep outside the gate for about eight more rounds**. The party is inside the fortress, heading down a tunnel past a ruined east hall that reeks of death.

## The party
- **Razageth** (Mike): dragonborn sorcerer (lightning), level 4. He has Spell Sniper, Suggestion, Command, Witch Bolt, Chromatic Orb, Shield, Mage Hand and Alter Self. His shard boosts his damage and Arcana but hits him back with psychic damage. Cryovain has his time-travel wrist guard. He holds the Potion of Greater Healing (the party knows) and is now **attuned to the 13-card deck**: he draws three, Jeff names the faces, and he picks one blind, for a result that could be great or terrible. Disguised as **Annatar**, he paid Adabra 100 gp and has about 25 gp left. **2 evil points.**
- **Tanner** (Cam): gnome barbarian 2 / bard 2, level 4, max HP 39. He killed Harbin and rescued **Penny**, who is with him. He has 125 gp and a Potion of Healing. Cam missed 11/15 entirely, so Tanner and Penny were off-table and will rejoin wherever the party is. **2 evil points.**
- **Aracheus** (Thomas): acid dragonborn druid, level 4, with his pet spider Varasst. He has Shadow Touched, Spike Growth, Flame Blade, Wrathful Smite, Misty Step, Sleep and Wild Shape (cat, giant badger). He can craft potions with his herbalism kit and is brewing a **Potion of Cold Resistance** (five days of work). He has about 115 gp. **2 evil points.**
- **Strider** (Brendan): tiefling ranger, level 4, with Skulker, Hail of Thorns, Goodberry and Thaumaturgy, plus his dog **Matt Boland**. His true name is Mike Boland, and his signet ring smites when he says it. He just noticed his **Cloak of Billowing**. He carries all the party's rock phones, has a Potion of Healing and about 115 gp, and earned a third evil point for stabbing a refugee (the point stands even though Adabra revived him). **3 evil points.**
- Each evil point is −1 on persuasion-type Charisma rolls. Killing or harming innocents adds points, and saving innocents removes them.
- Each PC will choose an armory weapon next session and has one ration.

## Key people
- **Cryovain**: young white dragon who hates dragonborn and wants their gold. Razageth's Command sent him off, but he came back at Axeholm and is now asleep at the gate. His lair holds Razageth's wrist guard and the party's old gold.
- **Gorrin**: the stone goliath the party hired with the Sun God for one dragon quest at 100 gp each. He summoned the party to Axeholm ("might be a little haunted"), came there with a group of people, and broke in. Now he won't pick up, and Strider has threatened him by voicemail.
- **The Sun God**: glowing spear fighter, hired alongside Gorrin. Not seen since 11/2.
- **Adabra Gwynn**: herbalist at the Umbrage Hill tower, sheltering Phandalin's refugees. She's the only source of healing potions. She threw Strider out and revived the refugee he stabbed, but she sold Annatar the cold-resistance ingredients.
- **Linene Graywind**: armory owner in Phandalin who told the party to take weapons and leave.
- **Alderleaf** ("Dragon Bait"): halfling farmer who escaped the shed. Strider has his shirt and suspects Halia helped him.
- **Halia Thornton**: Miners' Exchange boss. She vanished after the party paraded Harbin's head, and the bounty is probably not cleared.
- **Mark Boland**: Strider's father, a three-armed assassin. Jeff: *"You were deceived."*
- **Harbin Wester**: Phandalin's townmaster, killed by Tanner on 11/2.

## Sessions
- **The Dwarf Temple (2026-08-30):** The party cleared orcs and ochre jellies and killed their dwarf guides. Prying out the green gem blew up a statue.
- **Green Gems and a Bounty (2026-09-06):** They took 15 green gems. Halia warned them about Harbin's 500 gp bounty. Tanner turned himself in, then escaped.
- **The Manticore and the Vault (2026-09-24):** The Exchange vault break-in failed. Razageth and Aracheus paid off a manticore at Adabra's.
- **Cryovain Takes His Toll (2026-10-04):** Cryovain ate their gold and took the wrist guard. Alderleaf was kidnapped. The party took Halia's deal.
- **Strider's Father (2026-10-18):** The hidden guard was Mark Boland, and Strider spared him. Mark and the Sun God knocked out the party.
- **Jailbreak and Fire (2026-10-25):** The party escaped Harbin's prison, killed Maveth and set the hall on fire. Everyone reached level 4.
- **Baby in the Chimney (2026-11-02):** Tanner killed Harbin and rescued Penny. They looted 500 gp and the deck and hired Gorrin and the Sun God. Cryovain arrived for their gold.
- **The Dragon Sleeps at Axeholm (2026-11-15):** Razageth Commanded Cryovain away. The party hid in the Exchange, where the deck was attuned and the vault proved magically sealed. They looted ruined Phandalin and failed with Adabra as Strider but succeeded as Annatar. At Axeholm, Aracheus put Cryovain to sleep.
