## Recap

**Level-ups:** Everyone had long rested while knocked out. Strider and Tanner reached level 4. Strider took the Skulker feat, which gives him 10 ft of blindsight, and learned Hail of Thorns and Goodberry. Jeff leveled Tanner into his second bard level (Jack of All Trades, Expertise and one more spell). Cam can change Jeff's placeholder picks.

**The cell:** The party woke up in the basement prison of the Townmaster's Hall. They were chained to the wall and gagged, and their gear was piled in a chest in the corner. A goliath jailer, Gorrin, stood watch. Harbin Wester came down, frail and half-burned: *"Finally, I've got you."* He demanded to know why they'd been terrorizing him. He told Aracheus not to gaslight him: *"You guys have literally lit my house on fire."* He told Strider that his own father sold him out, took the reward and left town right away. Strider: *"Speak not of my father, you coward! Release my shackles and face me."* Harbin left them to "rot in this prison."

**The escape:** Penny was gone. Outside in the bushes, Varasst reached Aracheus telepathically and saw no guards. Aracheus Wild Shaped into a cat, slipped his cuffs and clawed everyone's gags out, scratching Razageth a few times along the way. The goliath noticed, and the party claimed the missing prisoner had run upstairs. Razageth's first Suggestion failed. The goliath called upstairs on his rock phone. Razageth's second Suggestion worked. The goliath called off the alarm, unlocked their cell, dropped his keys and rock phone, locked himself in the other cell and went to sleep. The cat brought the keys over, and everyone got free and got their gear back. Strider took the rock phone.

**Goliath fight:** Strider threw his voice outside with Thaumaturgy, which drew a second goliath out of the hall and gave the party time to put their armor on. The goliath came back, grew to double size and fought. His skin turns to stone to blunt hits. Razageth's Witch Bolt broke his concentration on Suggestion, so the caged goliath woke up and fired a crossbow through the bars. Razageth's crit Chromatic Orb, boosted by his shard, nearly killed the big goliath, and the orb leapt to the caged one. Aracheus finished the big one with Flame Blade and took off the top of his head.

**Fire:** The party left the caged goliath locked in. They searched the basement and found nothing, then threw the desk lantern into the hay bed. Jeff gave them about 20 rounds before the whole hall burns. Varasst rejoined them on the first floor. The quest board, a corner and the kitchen (food scraps) turned up nothing. Upstairs on the top floor, Varasst crept around a corner and came face to face with the **Sun God**, who recognized him. Initiative was rolled. The party argued between fighting and running out to bar the door, and the session ended there with about 16 rounds before the flames reach them. Tanner listened for Penny in a side room and heard nothing.
