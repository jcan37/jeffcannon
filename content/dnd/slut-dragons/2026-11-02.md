## Recap

**Back in the burning hall:** Only three rounds (18 seconds) had passed since Tanner lit the basement hay. Jeff put the fire at ten rounds per floor. Aracheus swapped Entangle for Create or Destroy Water so he could douse the fire later if needed. Tanner's placeholder level-4 spell is Tasha's Hideous Laughter.

**The Sun God runs:** After a reroll of initiative, Aracheus hit the Sun God with Flame Blade (9 fire) and Wrathful Smite that frightened him (2 necrotic). The frightened Sun God swung three times at disadvantage and landed one spear hit (7 piercing). Then he fled into the bathroom and locked the door.

**Looting the top floor:** Razageth crit a Strength check and ripped open a locked chest: **500 gp**, 125 each. The bedside drawer held **two Potions of Healing and a Potion of Greater Healing**. Razageth handed the regular ones to Strider and Tanner and jokingly announced he was keeping the Greater for himself (the party knows he has it). On a second look at the desk, Razageth found a **magical deck of 13 cards**. He looked through it without drawing and pocketed it while Strider tickled him for it. Varasst crawled under the bathroom door and found it empty with the window open. Aracheus picked the lock (20). The Sun God had jumped out the window (2 falling damage). From the roof, Varasst saw that the hall's front door was now open.

**Harbin in the chimney:** Razageth dashed downstairs and met two figures coming up from the basement: the Sun God and Gorrin, the charred goliath jailer. Back upstairs, Varasst heard a cough from the bedroom chimney and found **Harbin, blackened, hiding in it with Penny and his musket**. Tanner dashed up alone. Harbin: *"Don't come any closer. I'll hurt your baby."* He offered to give Penny back if they put the fire out and left town for good. Tanner told him the "miner chick" was trying to overthrow him. Harbin: *"I knew that bitch was trying to cross me."* Tanner sat on the chest and refused to leave without Penny. Harbin fired and missed, then squirmed up the chimney. Varasst bit him and poisoned him.

**Downstairs fight:** Aracheus cast Spike Growth on the two guards and drank a potion (+8). Gorrin hit him for 13. The Sun God shield-bashed him prone (8), which broke Spike Growth, then speared him for 9. **Aracheus dropped unconscious.** The Sun God lit his 10-ft glow, speared Matt Boland for 12 radiant and ran upstairs. Every opportunity attack missed. Razageth's level-2 Chromatic Orb hit Gorrin for 19 thunder. The bounce to the Sun God missed when he raised his AC. Strider shot Gorrin (10, bloodied), said *"My name is Mike Boland"* for his signet-ring smite, and Matt Boland bit the bloodied Sun God for 9.

**Harbin dies:** Tanner threw his trident up the chimney with a Reckless Attack (16, 12 damage). Harbin fell, Tanner dodged him, and Harbin landed on the trident, which went through his head. Tanner: *"Where's your rocket shoes now?"* **He took Penny back unharmed.** He carried Harbin's head downstairs on the trident. The Sun God and Gorrin stopped fighting: *"We're only here for money."* The party hired them for one quest (kill the dragon) at 100 gp each, and Gorrin gave them a rock phone to call them.

**Outside:** The townsfolk recognized the wanted party walking out of another burning building with the townmaster's head. Panic and looting broke out. Tanner stuck the head on a stake outside the hall. Halia stared at them from a distance (*"This is not what we agreed to"*). When they went to the Exchange she had vanished. Jeff: *"There's a good chance you never see her again."* Then the encounter roll came up and **Cryovain swooped down in front of them**, smelling their new gold. He wants all of it. He hates the dragonborn even more, called Tanner a chicken nugget and doesn't know or care who Harbin was. The session ended there. **Aracheus is still unconscious inside the burning hall.** Jeff: Varasst will bite him awake next time and he'll crawl out.
