## Recap

**Back on their feet:** Tanner and Aracheus were down after the statue explosion, and Razageth and Strider faced a full-health ochre jelly. Strider got Aracheus up with Cure Wounds, and Razageth stabilized Tanner. Tanner's reckless attack split the jelly in two, and one half knocked him out again until Varasst's Rejuvenate brought him back. Razageth spent his last spell slot on an ice Chromatic Orb that killed one jelly, bounced, and froze the other dead. The statue room held no treasure at all.

**Orc reinforcements:** Tanner declared he was done with the dungeon and headed out alone, straight into three orcs. The "something" from the earlier long rest was an orc scout that had found its dead war party and gone for help. It had nothing to do with the dwarves. Tanner held the cave mouth on about 1 HP. Aracheus melted him a little with an acid breath line through the orcs, then healed him with Healing Word. Penny gave enemies disadvantage against Tanner for a minute. Strider set one orc on fire with Fire Bolt, Varasst poisoned another, and Tanner finished them off.

**Treasure and rest:** A secret door in the northeast pillar of the main hall held 15 green gemstones, which went into the party pool. The party long rested in the cave and ate their last rations. The quest is complete, so everyone levels up before next session. They left in disguise: dwarf clothes for Tanner and Strider, orc clothes for Razageth and Aracheus.

**Halia Thornton:** Outside Phandalin, a woman in the bushes warned them away from town. Tanner threw his trident at her, and Aracheus took the hit. She had put them in that prison and left the door unlocked on purpose. The party is wanted, with a 500 gp bounty for whoever burned Harbin Wester's house, posted by Harbin himself. She pointed them to Adabra Gwynn at Umbrage Hill for healing potions and floated working together to replace the mayor. Tanner decided to turn himself in, have the reward sent to "Mike Boland," and break out later. He turned down Halia's offer of a disguise. She vanished, leaving one ration each for the other three.

**Tanner in Phandalin:** At the Miner's Exchange, Tanner asked about the reward for "that gang of miscreants," then ripped off his bloody dwarf outfit: *"Gentlemen, take me away."* That was when he learned the bounty money comes from Harbin. Harbin is alive. Tanner broke his restraints and ran, telling the party over the Sending Stone. A big guard with a spear chased him from the town marshal's hall. Tanner lost him in the alleys and dove into a haystack at Alderleaf Farm, where he is hidden for now.
