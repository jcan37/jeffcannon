## Recap

The party split up outside Phandalin. Razageth and Aracheus (with Varasst) headed to Umbrage Hill for healing potions. Strider and Tanner went into town to see Halia Thornton at the Miner's Exchange and plot against Harbin Wester.

**In town:** Halia wanted them to deal with Harbin at night and to get potions from Adabra Gwynn first. They insisted on the cellar, and she locked them downstairs. They tried the magic vault, but thieves' tools did nothing and the electrified lock shocked them both for 10 lightning damage. Things escalated into a fight. Halia nearly killed Strider in one hit (9 piercing + 21 poison, knocked unconscious) and poisoned Tanner badly. She locked the Exchange door. Tanner dragged Strider to the basement and revived him with Aracheus's leftover Goodberries. Halia eventually left, saying they were "the wrong people to help me." The pair slipped away to the southern haystacks to lie low. The vault was never opened.

**At Umbrage Hill:** A manticore was battering the door of Adabra Gwynn's stone windmill. It wanted 50 gp, since a dragon had pushed it out of its lair. The party faked a payment and cast Sleep, and a brutal fight followed. Razageth went down once, and Aracheus used Land's Aid while Varasst bit and healed. Partway through, the manticore reopened talks. In the end they paid the 50 gp (25 each) and it flew off. A grateful Adabra gave each of them a Potion of Healing, fed them, and let them sleep on the first floor. She refused to go back to Phandalin but offered a signed note for Harbin instead. Extra potions cost 50 gp each, and an attempt to talk her down on price failed.
