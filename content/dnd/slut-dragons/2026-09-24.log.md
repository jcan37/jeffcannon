## Scene 1: The party splits
- Strider insisted on going into town to back up Tanner. Cam: *"I gotta know what's in that safe."* Tanner also wants Phandalin as a home base again, *"and that's not gonna happen if Harvin's still alive."*

- **The split:** Aracheus and Razageth would go to Adabra Gwynn for potions. Strider would join Tanner in town. The potion pair would wait **one hour** and then leave.
- Before Strider left, Aracheus cast **Goodberry** and gave him **six berries**. *"I approve of what you're doing, but I don't want two of you to die."*
- Ruling: Strider can choose which beast he summons; a hell hound/hell hawk came up *(details uncertain)*.
- It was midday, roughly afternoon. Over the stone, Tanner sent: *"Change of plans, Miners' Exchange, empty, meet there first."*
- **New item for Strider:** a **Cloak of Billowing**, another family heirloom. It's purely cosmetic: as a bonus action it billows dramatically. He put the hood up.

## Scene 2: The Miners' Exchange and the electrified vault
- **Tanner** used **Disguise Self** to look like a portly maid. He got in by claiming he was there to "clean out the bolts." Jeff called for **Deception**.
- **Evil points (updated rule):** they're now **tracked per character**, and everyone currently has **2**. **Every Charisma check gets −(evil points), except Intimidation, which is improved.**
- Tanner rolled 6 + 2 − 2 and failed. Halia: *"You can't even fool me with that disguise."*
- **Halia Thornton** ("Holly Thornton"): *"I run this miners' exchange."* She told them to keep the disguise up and come back after dark, when it would *"maybe be a good time to try to get rid of Harbin."*
- Tanner asked about the vault. Halia: *"A ton of money."* She wouldn't open it. Tanner shoved her aside, and she stayed calm.
- She urged them to visit Adabra Gwynn first for potions, but offered the **cell downstairs** if they insisted on staying. She warned that she would have to shut the door.
- **Strider's Insight** (+6 with advantage, 16): **she's honest and genuinely trying to help.** They went down willingly.
- **In the basement** there's Aracheus's old cell, the **bank vault**, and the stairs up.
  - Tanner tried **Silent Image** to project his disguise upstairs. Ruling: no, he has to see the spot, and the door is closed.
  - Tanner put on **splint armor** from the party inventory. Someone said it came "from the guard that we killed" *(origin uncertain; not shown on any tape)*. It took his AC to 19, but he lacks proficiency, so he has **disadvantage on Dex** while wearing it.
  - Strider learned **Mending**. He also has **Hellish Rebuke** from his tiefling heritage at level 3: one free use, then with spell slots.
  - **The vault is magically locked and electrified.** Tanner's first attempt shocked him for 10 lightning (heavily reduced by some modifier; it was described as about 1 net) *(uncertain)*. Then Tanner held the vault "to take the shock" while Strider picked it with thieves' tools. **Both took 10 lightning.** Jeff: *"It's a magic lock. You need magic to break it."* Cam: *"If he rolls a nat twenty, we're supposed to be able to do anything we want."* Jeff: no.
  - They decided to wait for nightfall instead of tunnelling through the wall ("Shawshank").

## Scene 3: Umbrage Hill and the manticore
- With no word from the others after an hour, Aracheus and Razageth headed off. They mock-mourned *"for the recorder."* Jeff rolled 2d20 for an encounter (1 and 9) and nothing happened.
- **Lore Jeff read:** "Umbridge Hill" *(as heard; likely Umbrage Hill)* got its name when two feuding dwarf clans fought a pitched battle on top of it. *"The cause of their umbrage is lost to time;"* only the cairns of the dead remain.
- **The scene:** an old stone windmill on the slope, inside an iron (farm-style, roughly 5-ft) fence. A large winged monster with a spiky tail was trying to knock in the door. A woman at a second-floor window called *"A little help!"* Aracheus signalled her to stay quiet.
- **Ruling: ritual spells.** Speak with Animals and Find Familiar can be cast as rituals in a minute without using a slot.
- **Talking to it.** Aracheus: *"Brother, what irks you?"* The **manticore**: *"We can just use the common tongue if you want."*
  - It's hungry: *"If you don't have any food, then maybe you guys are food."*
  - It said **the dragon pushed it out of its lair** and it has been foraging since. It asked for **50 gold** to leave the woman alone.
  - Aracheus asked it to join the hunt for the dragon (*"I know what it is like to lose your home"*). It cackled: *"Me and you are not the same at all."*
  - *"50 gold now, or I'm gonna fuck you up."*
  - Perception (23): it's large, horse-sized, with a lion's head and a spiky tail.
- **The ambush.** Aracheus pretended to hand over gold and both sides rolled initiative. Razageth and Aracheus rolled poorly. Ruling: the surprised manticore rolled with disadvantage and got 13.
  - **Aracheus cast Sleep** (Wis 12). It failed the first save and was incapacitated. Ruling: Sleep is a saving-throw spell, so range doesn't impose disadvantage. Any damage would wake it.
  - Aracheus used bonus-action **Shillelagh**. **Ruling:** only one spell slot per turn, though a cantrip plus a leveled spell is fine. He couldn't also summon Flame Blade that turn.
  - **Razageth** cast **Mirror Image** (three duplicates; each hit is redirected on a d6 roll of 3 or more).
  - The manticore **made its second save and woke up**: *"So I guess we're not making a deal then."* It fired three tail spikes: two missed Aracheus and one hit a duplicate.
  - Aracheus rolled a 1 with shillelagh, then stowed his staff and summoned **Flame Blade**. **Varasst** landed a poison bite (17) for 5 and **poisoned** it.
  - Razageth used innate sorcery and a sorcerous burst (lightning) for 1 damage.
  - The poisoned manticore hit Aracheus with a claw for 6. Aracheus swung Flame Blade (an attack action, not a new slot) and missed with an 11. He **Misty Stepped** 30 ft behind the fence. Ruling: teleporting provokes no opportunity attack. Varasst used **Rejuvenate** on him and he was back to full.
  - The manticore flew off, came back, and killed both remaining duplicates. A third spike hit Razageth for 11. Razageth chose not to use Shield.
  - **Aracheus critted with Ice Knife** for 9 piercing. The manticore saved against the cold burst. Varasst Rejuvenated Razageth for 17.
  - **Razageth** hit with **Chromatic Orb (lightning)** for 11 and activated his **"Shard of Dimensior"** *(name as heard; spelling uncertain)* for an extra 1d8. That forced a **DC 15 Cha save**, which he failed, and **he took 11 psychic**. A voice in his mind said: *"All you have accomplished, all you have sacrificed, will have been for nothing… I have watched you. I have tasted your essence. It won't be long now."* The manticore took 15 total and was **bloodied**.
  - The manticore lowered its price to **25, then 30 gold**. Razageth tried to intimidate it: *"Wrong answer."* Two spikes hit Razageth for **16 piercing, knocking him unconscious.** The manticore flew around behind the windmill.
  - Aracheus couldn't reach Razageth for a Medicine check. He used his level-3 **Land's Aid** feature (spending one wild shape), chose **no damage targets**, and **healed Razageth 9**. Jeff allowed it. Razageth cast **False Life** and was back to 20/28.
  - The manticore returned and threw spikes. Razageth Shielded one and **Aracheus took 21 piercing, dropping to 1 HP.** Ruling: half cover is +2 AC.
  - Aracheus used his last slot on another **Ice Knife** (21 to hit) for 3. The manticore saved against the burst. **Varasst's third and last Rejuvenate** brought Aracheus back to 18.
  - Razageth moved and used his last level-2 slot on **Chromatic Orb** for 10, curving it around the windmill.
  - The manticore, nearly dead, said: *"I can do this all day… my price now is fifty gold."*
- **They paid.** *"You are a worthy adversary and you have earned this fifty gold. Now begone… lest you face our wrath."* **Each dropped 25 gp.** It scooped the gold in its talons: *"Pleasure doing business with you."* Aracheus admitted the real motive: *"My biggest motivator right now is not wanting to fucking deal with Cam and Brendan."*

If you can make Harbin disappear, I can try to smooth things over with the people in Phandalin."* Asked why: *"Because he's a giant douche."* She lost patience: *"Just get the fuck out of here."*

- **Strider** attacked with his short sword and missed (5). His hell hound wasn't summoned.
- **Halia** drew a **poisoned short sword**. Her first strike missed Strider. **The second hit for 9 piercing + 21 poison, and Strider dropped unconscious.** She hit Tanner for **7 piercing + 19 poison** and poisoned him, leaving him on about 6 HP. *"You're so fucking annoying."* She then **disengaged and locked them all in** the exchange.

## Rulings summary
- Evil points are per character (all at 2): −EP to every Charisma check except Intimidation, which is improved.
- One spell slot per turn (a cantrip plus a leveled spell is fine). An attack with an already-summoned Flame Blade doesn't use a slot.
- Ritual casting: one minute, no slot.
- Misty Step doesn't provoke.
- Ranged attacks within 5 ft are at disadvantage. Weapon ranges are normal/long, with long at disadvantage.
- Half cover is +2 AC.
- Wearing armor you're not proficient in gives disadvantage on Dex.
- Surprise: the surprised side rolls initiative with disadvantage.
- The vault needs magic. Thieves' tools won't work.
- Land's Aid can be used purely to heal.
