## Recap
Jeff opened by reading an AI recap of 9/24. Razageth and Aracheus beat the manticore down and paid it off, then slept and ate at Adabra's windmill with one Potion of Healing each. Tanner and Strider were nearly killed by Halia and long-rested in the south haystacks. The party still holds the **15 green gems** from the dwarf temple. Jeff mentioned that **dragons love loot**, which could be a lever, but it would take *"a shit ton."*

## Scene 1: Rests, rations, and brewing
- **Rations:** nobody had any in inventory. Jeff ruled that **one Goodberry counts as one ration**.
- **Short-rest hit dice:** Tanner has 2d12 + 1d8 (barbarian/bard), Aracheus 3d8, Razageth 3d6, Strider 3d10. Add your Con modifier to each die.
  - Tanner rolled 21 and Strider rolled 17 + 6. Both were back to full. Razageth healed to 18 (full) at Adabra's.
- **Downtime:** Jeff explained crafting, spell scrolls, and similar options. He mentioned his own character in another campaign earned 200 gp by enriching a farm with a spell. Characters get feats every four levels.
- **Aracheus brews a Potion of Healing.** He has an herbalism kit (from his criminal background). He bought **25 gp of materials from Adabra** and worked for about 4 hours. Jeff ruled it **halfway done**; finish later and track it in notes. Mike also asked Adabra's house for a Survival-style check for useful things. She only has potion materials and her stock.

## Scene 2: Razageth gets them kicked out of the windmill
- With their gold now below what they had told her, Razageth tried to **intimidate Adabra** for a 10 gp discount: *"You see what we just did to that giant, crazy animal monster? What do you think we can do to you?"* He rolled **12, failed**.
- Adabra: *"I think you guys should leave now. I actually don't think you guys are allowed to stay here now."* **She won't sell them potions anymore.**
- Aracheus knocked again and apologized. Because he wasn't the one who threatened her, she sold rations at a "rude" markup: **10 rations at 2 gp each = 20 gp**, split 10/10 between Razageth and Aracheus. Aracheus seems to have held them at first and later shared them out *(exact split uncertain)*.

## Scene 3: Tanner and Strider kidnap the farmer
- In the haystack, Tanner rolled Perception 3 and saw no dragon in the sky. *"It's a really peaceful evening."*
- **The shed:** farming tools. Tanner took a **pitchfork** as a second "trident."
- **The farmhouse next door:** Tanner knocked. His Disguise Self had already worn off (1 hour). The farmer asked why he was covered in blood and said Harbin has *"been a good mayor."* Then he recognized them from the wanted posters: *"You're the gnome, and you're the tiefling… I don't see the other two Dragonborns."* Everyone in town knows they burned Harbin's house.
- Strider slipped in through a back window. Tanner punched through the screen door. They **gagged** the farmer and threatened him with a sword. Strider played good cop: *"Harbin sent us to take out the dragon."*
- Perception: Tanner 16, Strider 19. They heard **running footsteps** with less than a minute to spare.
- They raided the pantry and got **one ration each**, then **dragged the farmer out**. *"No, no, please, sir, don't. I'll give you whatever you want."*
- **Strider summoned his beast companion Matt Boland**, a dog. It held off the pursuer, one of the **"stone goliath" veteran guards**, and **was killed**. Ruling (Primal Companion): he can restore it within an hour with a touch and a spell slot. They missed the window, so **it returns on his next long rest**.

## Scene 4: Cryovain
- Jeff had been rolling every time the party travelled. On leaving the windmill, Aracheus and Razageth heard a roar overhead. **Cryovain, the white dragon**, landed in front of them, *"licking his lips… he wants to eat you, and he wants to take all your money."* Aracheus's Insight was 4. Jeff called the dragon cocky.
- Initiative: both rolled 9 and the dragon went first. Razageth took a short rest just before and was at full HP. Aracheus was at 18/22. Both were almost out of spell slots.
- **Talking:** *"Hark, brother!"* Cryovain: *"You're not my brother. You are a disgusting spawn of dragons."* He wants *"money and food, and it seems like you guys might satisfy both."* Razageth tried to redirect him to Adabra's riches and pulled *"what's that?"* Deception 14 − 2 for evil points. **Failed.**
- **Icy breath** (30 ft):
  - Varasst has Magic Resistance (advantage on the save) and rolled a **nat 20**. **New house rule:** a natural 20 on a save against a half-damage effect means **no damage**.
  - **Razageth took 44 cold. Aracheus took 22 cold. Both dropped.**
- Varasst tried to hide under Razageth (Stealth 9) and was spotted. *"Spider, you put some respect in your goddamn mouth."*
- **Death saves.** House rule confirmed: a nat 20 counts as two successes and a nat 1 as two failures.
- **Cryovain took all of Aracheus's gold (49 gp)**, then **all of Razageth's gold (70 gp)**, and *swallowed it*.
- Varasst dashed toward Phandalin to find Tanner and Strider. Cryovain ignored him.
- **Aracheus** stabilized and came back on **1 HP**. Razageth was at 2 failures and 1 success. Cryovain started **eating Razageth**.
- **Aracheus** used his last wild shape on **Land's Aid**. Cryovain saved at disadvantage (sneak attack, since he thought Aracheus was down) and rolled a **critical failure**: **8 necrotic**. Razageth healed **8** and came back up, **grappled in Cryovain's mouth**. The dragon was biting at the time device on his wrist.
- Aracheus used bonus-action Shillelagh: *"Hey, big dummy, over here!"* Ruling: grappled means speed 0, disadvantage on attacks, and a save to escape.
- **Razageth** converted **2 sorcery points into a level-1 slot** (bonus action) and cast **Command: "Flee."** Cryovain rolled **3** and failed. He let go and dashed 160 ft. Then he **flew off**, judging them not worth eating.
- **Loss:** as he fled he **ripped off Razageth's time-travel wrist guard** (not the shard). It isn't destroyed; Cryovain has it. Mike: *"That's my whole lore… now that's a real motivation for me to kill this dragon."*

## Scene 5: Back together, then apart again
- The two pairs met on the road within about 30 minutes. They introduced the farmer as a *"Harbin sympathizer."* He gave his name as **Alderleaf**, *"the town farmer."* Tanner calls him **"Dragon Bait."* He has no gold.
- **Jeff: Razageth and Aracheus are now level 4.** Tanner and Strider stay level 3 *"until the next thing happens."*
- With no next lead, Tanner pushed for Adabra's place. Then Tanner announced he had told **Varasst to go to Harbin's house**, saying Aracheus would meet him there. Deception 12 + 2 − 2 = 12. Jeff: Varasst *"is not very intelligent… he's not stupid either."* **Varasst believed him and headed into town.**
- Jeff: *"Guys, are you guys splitting up again? Can you guys like fucking play this together?"*
- Everyone followed Aracheus. They called it **"Saving Private Varasst."**
- **Alderleaf was tied up in the shed** with the pitchfork through the back of his collar and a sock in his mouth. They found **three coils of rope** there and took them. Tanner kissed him on the forehead: *"We'll come back for you."*

## Scene 6: Into town; Halia hides them again
- Aracheus pulled up his hood and hunched down: **Stealth 20**. Jeff had a 10 as the target. Nobody noticed him as dark fell.
- Near the Miners' Exchange he reached Varasst telepathically: *"Hey Thomas, I'm waiting at Harbin's burned-down house. Tanner told me to meet you here."* *"Tanner's not a nice guy, and he was playing a prank."* Varasst made his way over.
- **Tanner started screaming "Intruder! Intruder!"** *("Trust me, this has to happen.")* Guards' footsteps came running.
- **Halia** flung open the exchange door: *"What the fuck are you doing? Get in here now!"* Tanner urged her to fight Harbin tonight. Aracheus tried to Persuade Tanner and rolled 19 (with advantage). Halia: *"I can't be seen doing this mission with you guys… but I will try to help you any way I can,"* including **tools or weapons**.
- Someone knocked Tanner out and he was hauled inside *(who did it, and how, is uncertain)*. They threw him down the basement stairs (Str save). Halia **closed the door but didn't lock it** and told Tanner to shut up.
- A guard knocked: *"I heard some screaming."* Halia sent him off to check the **south wall**. Then: *"I think this is the time for you."*
- **Short rest:** Razageth got his sorcery points back. Aracheus healed 3d8 for 8.
- **Level 4:**
  - Aracheus: **+2 Wis**, max HP **31**, a new level-2 slot and a new spell.
  - Razageth: +1 Con and +1 Dex (Jeff suggested Cha instead; changeable before next session), 4 sorcery points, a new level-2 slot.
  - Varasst back to full.
  - Tanner and Strider *"feel envy for having sat in a fucking haystack."*
- **Halia's deal, stated plainly:** *"Once you take out Harbin, I will be the mayor of this town, and I will make sure that you guys are no longer wanted."* She can't be involved herself.
- **The vault:** it isn't opened with a key. *"You cast a spell that made yourself look like a fat woman. Do you really believe that you need a physical object to open that door?"* It takes **a spell**.

Jeff wouldn't say how many guards there are. Reminder from the players: **Harbin has a gun** (a musket) and shot at them before.

- **Aracheus cast Sleep** (his last level-2 slot) and **the guard saved**. Initiative was rolled: Aracheus about 14, Strider 14, Razageth about 13, Tanner about 10 *(exact order uncertain)*, guard 14.
- **Reveal:** a **second, hidden guard** rolled **25** for initiative. Jeff showed the image of the outside of Harbin's house he had shared weeks ago. The figure in it **has three arms**, and Jeff told Brendan *"you recognize him because he looks like you."*
- The session ended there. The party is low on resources.

## Rulings and house rules
- One Goodberry equals one ration.
- Hit dice plus Con modifier on a short rest.
- Nat 20 on a save against a half-damage effect means no damage. Nat 20 on a death save is two successes; nat 1 is two failures.
- Grappled: speed 0, disadvantage on attacks, save to escape.
- Command: Flee lasts one turn.
- Converting sorcery points takes a bonus action (2 points for a level-1 slot).
- Primal Companion: restore within an hour (touch plus a spell slot), otherwise it returns on a long rest.
- Evil points: −2 on Deception and Persuasion. Intimidation is the exception.
- Disguise Self lasts 1 hour.
