## Recap

The Northern Bridge fell quiet. Three of the five knights who stormed the front were dead. Brad's Prayer of Healing patched everyone up. Freyr found a **soul coin** sewn into the captain's coat. His invisible familiar found the far barricade abandoned in a hurry. In the supply crates were potions and a **Scroll of Daylight** altered to give a five-to-ten-second flash.

The knights brought the civilians over without **Apple**. **Senezhael** had come to claim her souls. Apple held her off so the others could run, and he fell under her daggers and spells. "This time he's not coming back. Not in that form." The party poured out wine for him.

**Ikaia** arrived, and the party gave him the bridge. The refugees agreed to scatter into western Elturel and meet later in the residential district under a new resistance mark. **Elias** stayed with Autumn, and **the Mountain** went with his knights. That leaves Freyr, Tink, Brad and Lulu. Ikaia offered a wish if they strip the sunlight barriers from **Lord Ottmar's** residence. The talks ended in a bigger bargain: the party will remove "anybody that opposes" Ikaia in exchange for his help once, at a time of their choosing, called with a **sapphire sending stone**. Freyr's Insight warned him that the resistance opposes Ikaia too. Two hours later they pulled the lanterns, and the mist rolled over the bridge.

The Ashen soldiers who had fled came back as a regiment staging at **the Maiden's Bridge** (location 10). With them were mages, a devil, and a bearded commander holding a spear that blazed like sunlight. Elias stopped Tink from killing Dain: he is really **Corin**. Brad drew **the Sun**. A voice said *"Fast, my child. Break the barrier,"* and the Companion's thunder covered them smashing a basement wall into the **sewers**. A former construction worker led everyone to a peaceful residential district. Everyone lost 5 corruption.

"New Elturel" looked calm, and nobody in it was armed. Freyr went alone with the bag of holding to an alchemist, who said old gold was no longer accepted. Freyr pocketed potions and a metal vial labeled **"Do not open,"** and then guards walked him off. Tink and Brad stood out as a tiefling and a dragonborn. When an innkeeper demanded their "first memory," armored men took them too. All three were led past the **Shield Hall** (location 13) to the devil-guarded **House of New Names** (location 11).

Freyr's Fog Cloud started a breakout, but nobody had armor on. Tink was caught in the chase, told the others to run, and drew **the Hanged Man**. He cut down soldiers and devils until three black arrows from a lone figure dropped him to his knees. Freyr and Brad turned back anyway. Freyr's **Chromatic Orb** crit bounced through the Ashen ranks, and Tink killed a devil. The session ended with Tink and Brad down, Tink on a yellow card, and Freyr alone in his fog.
