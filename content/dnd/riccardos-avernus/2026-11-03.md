## Recap

The party chose to leave the keep and spent the morning getting ready to march out the front gate. Tink and Apple designed water "squirt guns" (a wine bottle plunged into a bucket of holy water). They built four in an hour and handed them to the squires, Elias and the cart. Edric explained that a bucket of water burns vampires like acid. They also learned that lesser vampires can't enter a home without an invitation, and the keep was still Edric's and the Mountain's home. Freyr cast Speak with Animals on the horses and took the rear on horseback. They planned the caravan's formations and horn signals. Brad prayed to Lathander and drew The Magician, Death *(uncertain)* and The Hermit. He chose **The Hermit**, and the Lantern of the Dawn's light grew to **20 feet**.

Then fog rolled across the bridge, and a cloaked envoy of **Ikaia** walked out of it alone. Brad's Divine Sense showed the man wasn't undead, but the fog behind him was full of undead. The envoy said the keep had broken its pact and judgment had come. Freyr bluffed that the lantern could fry them all (Deception 25), and **Ikaia himself** stepped out of the mist with his right hand, **Lady Seris**. He offered a deal: clear one of the bridges of every living thing and hand it to Lady Seris. Brad pushed for better terms (Persuasion 26). Ikaia agreed to spare everyone in the keep if they **gave up the keep** and **destroyed the lantern**. Tink asked Edric and the knights whether they would sacrifice themselves, but Brad wouldn't accept that. He used telepathy to force Edric's hand (29). Edric knelt, offered Ikaia his sword and said *"The keep is yours, my lord."* Brad flew out and dropped the Lantern of the Dawn into the lava, and every knight felt the shockwave. **Brad took 3 corruption** (now at 7).

The mist parted, and the caravan walked out under safe passage. They passed dead Ashen Guards in the streets. Apple prayed to Yondala and chose **The Chariot**. Elias thanked him, and Riccardo said it leads toward a greater destiny, but maybe also the ultimate sacrifice a leader makes. **Thomas** led them to **the Antlers**, a shuttered tavern at location 8 that is secretly a resistance safe house. Its owner, **Ressa**, still lives there. Inside they met the scout **Nella** and the frantic courier **Dane**. Dane says **Autumn**, Elias's daughter, freed him at the **Southern Bridge** but fell to the Ashen Guard there, and that she ordered everyone north. Nella says the Northern Bridge is crawling with Ashen soldiers and maybe mages. Ressa's silent "I'm sorry" made Elias cry. Brad introduced himself as **Broderick Reinhardt**, a knight of Lathander. The session ended there.
