## Recap

The fight for the Northern Bridge picked up where it stopped. A crowned figure climbed out of the void under the bridge and perched on the wall. His presence frightened everyone on the bridge. Under his cape was a horned helmet like the Witch-king's, and Brad recognized it. Behind the visor, his eyes burned bright yellow. He told **Silas**, still in giant-snake form, *"You have betrayed your patron, beast. You shall answer for your crimes."* Silas struck at him with impossible speed. The figure vanished, reappeared behind the snake, grabbed it by the neck, and both disappeared in a puff of green smoke. Silas's spirit serpents faded with him. He left behind **angel wing feathers**, but not the blinding white of a true angel's: these were cream or off-white with black stripes. With Spencer away, the party fought on without Silas or Apple.

The Ashen force was a captain, two sergeants, infantry and an **arcanist**, a mage with Counterspell, Hold Person, radiant bolts and a recharging spell of darkness tendrils. The arcanist Counterspelled Freyr's Find Familiar, his Fog Cloud and Tink's Darkness. Brad finally shook off Hold Person and then went down again and again. Freyr kept bringing him back with Misty Step and temporary HP. Tink tried to pole-vault over the enemy line with his halberd, failed, then grappled the paralyzed Brad and dragged him clear. When things looked hopeless, the party called **the Mountain** to their aid. His sweeps and shoulder charges broke the line. Brad landed a natural-20 smite on the captain for **40**. The arcanist's tendrils restrained Brad, Tink and the Mountain twice, and Brad's necklace kept him at 1 HP.

Freyr finally got his pseudodragon familiar, refilled a slot with his Rod of the Pact Keeper, and dropped **Hunger of Hadar** on the Ashen cluster. Blinded inside it, the arcanist couldn't find targets for his big spell. Freyr finished the arcanist with a **Booming Blade** from his staff. His body shattered, and a trapped soul screamed as it left him. The knights who had cleared the bridge entrance came back with losses. They can hold the center of the bridge, but they can't break the checkpoint on the far side. The session ended there.
