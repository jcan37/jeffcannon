## Recap

After a long rest on the stolen lava barge, the party opened its sealed compartment: a blood-stained feeding room with chains, with the windows blacked out in flammable wax. The barge hull is plated in infernal iron.

Joven, a former dock foreman, explained that vampires hold his wife and daughter at the Northern Dock (6A). The southern dock takes in supplies, the middle dock (6B) runs a civilian ferry, and the northern dock is military and sits next to the keep of the Knights of Lathander. The party sailed past the middle dock. The 27 refugees and Lulu hid in the sealed blood room, since vampires can't sense life through a fully sealed barrier.

At the Northern Dock the party posed as guards, and Freyr played the captain. Their story was that the slaughterhouse had been raided and they needed reinforcements. A vampire couldn't read Tink and asked who his master was. Tink said "Raphael," and combat broke out.

Tink's tarot (The Moon) revealed the layout: a locked tower holding a sending stone linked to the keep and the other docks, barracks, a quartermaster's office with a big chest and plate armor, and a mess hall. The boss, Dark Master Varex Sorgna, was hiding invisible on the tower. Several guards and a vampire ended up in the lava. Apple's Star tarot called down lightning that hit Sorgna and some of the party, and it killed Freyr's familiar. Brad prayed to Lathander and drew The World. His oath broke, and the vampires present turned human again, though they aren't friendly. Freyr reached the tower top and studied the sending stone. It has been corrupted to link to a different twin. Freyr prayed to Seraphina and drew Temperance: the vampires just want to feed.

The session ended mid-fight and mid-negotiation with Sorgna.
