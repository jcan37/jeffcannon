## Scene 6: Ikaia's deal

It's **Ikaia** himself, with the mist orbiting him. *"Speaking face to face is what gentlemen do, after all."* He has claims on every soul in the keep and the right to enter and take them all, but he has *"a more pressing matter."*

The party is *"extremely annoying, but capable,"* and none of them is undead. He wants them to **clear one of the bridges, removing every living being and anything that could make his passage harder, then relinquish it to Lady Cerys**. He doesn't need it destroyed.
- He jokes about the party destroying his "smoothie factory" and his "personal delivery driver" (allegedly). The table reminisces about the spoons guy, who never made a deal with Ikaia.
- Freyr notes that Ikaia hinted there's another lantern-like light on the bridge, which may be why he can't take it himself.
- Tink: their one goal is to take every innocent soul in the keep across.
- **Tink asks Edric and the Mountain over telepathy whether they'll sacrifice themselves**, since they made the deal and their souls are on the ledger: *"Are they knights or not?"* Riccardo: it would be Edric and every knight who knew, including the Mountain. Edric is frozen with fear. The Mountain is conflicted. He doesn't want to die, but he wants to save the people.
- **Brad refuses the sacrifice**, and Freyr backs Brad (*"I'm with Brad"*). Tink: *"Brad, this is the way the world works… I love that you have this idealistic view of the world."*

- **Ikaia's terms:** *"The keep is mine, but on one condition… You shall walk away with your lives, you and everybody within here."* The party must **relinquish the keep and renounce any rights to it, and destroy the lantern.**
- Apple and Tink are for the deal. Brad: *"I'm making the decision. It's not up to Edric anymore."* Over telepathy he tells Edric, *"It's either this or you're dead. Your choice."* That's Intimidation with advantage plus Freyr's Guidance d4: **29**.
- The party feels a wave of total loss through the telepathy, then hears the Mountain's scream. **Edric** walks down, orders the gate opened, kneels before Ikaia, and holds up his sword like a gift: *"The keep is yours, my lord. I relinquish the keep."* Ikaia pats his head with the sword and walks in with his arms spread, breathing deeply like he's entering his new home. Then he asks: *"Well, where's the lantern then?"*
- Tink rolls Insight on Ikaia: **23**. *"He keeps his word. The guy is nothing but a gentleman… beautiful, cunning and ruthless, but he gives his word."*
- **Brad** draws the lantern, bows to Ikaia (who bows back), spreads his wings (spending a first-level slot *"for drama"*), flies out over the river and drops it. Ikaia and Lady Cerys watch it fall onto the moving crust and melt into the lava. A boom like a sonic wave washes over everyone, and every knight feels it.
- **Brad takes 3 corruption** (mandatory) and is now at **7**. Nothing happens at 7 yet. Brad: *"I'm saving people by doing this."*
- Ikaia: *"Go get me the bridge."* The party has safe passage until then.

## Scene 8: The Antlers (Emberleaf)
- **Location 8.** The street of a tavern lies under drifting ash, and the mist is starting to seep in. The tavern's shutters are closed, heavy timbers bar the doors, and there's no sign above the entrance. Thomas leads them down an alley, through two backyards screened by hanging sheets, to a service door. A small cover slides open, and someone asks, *"Who sent you? Did anyone follow?"* They recognize Thomas: *"We've not heard from you in days."* Then: *"We don't have space for this many."*
- The four PCs, Elias, Thomas and **Brenna** go inside. The refugees camp in the alley and a building across the backyard, guarded by Edric and the knights. Edric *"looks like an empty man that has lost everything."*
- **Inside:** the room is crowded and warm, with a low fire, and smells of damp wool, boiled herbs and old beer. Freyr drinks some, and it's bitter. Beds line the walls, and the medicine crates are mostly empty. An **enormous pair of black antlers** hangs above the fireplace, which is where the tavern gets its name. Beneath them, swords are arranged with odd care: one points up, one hangs down, two are crossed with a strip of white cloth, one is snapped in half with amber-scarlet fabric around the hilt, and several hooks are empty. Freyr asks if any look magical. Not from sight, and he can check Arcana up close.
- A table holds maps and measuring tools. A **courier** with a crate shouts: *"They took me at the southern bridge. Autumn came for me. She fell getting us out. Her last orders were to take everyone north. We need to leave."* A **scout** blocks him: the Northern Bridge is crawling with soldiers. An **older woman** speaks quietly and the whole room stops: *"Nobody is moving until we know where we are going."*
- Riccardo shows NPC pictures and says they contain hints. There's no sign of Autumn.
- Tink casts Thaumaturgy and says they came because everyone is in danger. The scout gives her name as **Nella** and calls the courier **Dane**: *"Have you seen my report?"*
- Tink introduces himself and says they're from the surface (*"I'm from Baldur's Gate"*). Apple: *"I'm from the Shire. Your place kind of screwed me over, but I'm here to help anyway."* Apple warns they have hours before the vampires find this place, as happened at the butcher shop, the hospital and every dock.
- Thomas confirms he escaped the hospital. Nobody else from the hospital made it here, and Elias's heart sinks.
- **The map:** a map of eastern Elturel marked in code. The hospital is marked in red, and blue dots, some with an X, show locations. Dane wants to take "the old hospital lane," and Nella points to the crossed blades with white cloth: *"The hospital lane is closed. Look at the fucking map."* She looks between Dane and the weapon rack and accuses him of not having looked at either.
- **Tink grapples Dane** and elbows him in the stomach, then rolls Insight with Freyr's Guidance: **20**. Dane is fit, a bit malnourished and built to run. He is a genuine courier, not a fighter or a troublemaker. Tink puts him down.
- **Dane's story:** he was held at the **Southern Bridge**. Autumn got him out but fell, and the last thing he saw was guards swarming her. Her last order was to take everyone to the Northern Bridge. The Southern Bridge is the more heavily guarded of the two, he says.
- **Nella's report:** she was at the Northern Bridge a few hours ago. It is reinforced continuously, with barricades, Ashen patrols, soldiers and maybe magic users. The scouts lost contact with the south when everything happened. The Southern Bridge has stone guardhouses, checkpoints at both ends. Ashen carts load people, cross both bridges and come back empty.
- Apple rolls Insight on Dane about the Southern Bridge. He gets 12, then 14, then 17 with Guidance, and Riccardo calls each one a failure.
- **Ressa Thorne**, the older woman, owns the establishment, which used to be the Antlers. Her grandfather and father lived there, so Tink reasons that's why vampires can't enter. She says vampires have never troubled them. They worry about the **Ashen government**, which hunts them, and the place stays hidden because it's so close to the bridges that the government passes it by. Some of the hot-headed resisters fight, and some don't come back. She's an innkeeper by trade and a healer by necessity. The medical crates are mostly bandages, and the larder wall, once full of cured meat and bread, is nearly bare.
- Ressa asks why she should trust them. **Thomas** vouches for them and points to Elias as Autumn's father. Ressa silently mouths *"I'm sorry,"* and Elias starts to cry. Nella: this is their safe house in the north, and as far as they know the vampires stay on the docks.
- Tink explains that the keep's people and roughly 60 refugees are outside, and they're crossing into Ashen territory *"and we don't care how many of them we have to kill."* Apple says they know a potential way home. Ressa doesn't want people getting false hope. Tink: *"This guy is literally blessed by a god… a godly lizard."*
- **Brad** brings Brenna (dressed as a knight) and introduces himself: *"The name is Broderick Reinhardt… a knight of Lathander, and I promise to protect every living soul in this city."* Ressa Thorne: *"Then help us."*
