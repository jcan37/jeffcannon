# Riccardo's Avernus: the story so far

## Where things stand
The party is **level 7**. **Tink is dead**: the Ashen hanged him in the courtyard of the **House of New Names** (location 11) on 11/18, and **Raphael** has claimed his sword. The survivors are **Freyr** and **Brad**, plus **Silas**, who was found in a cell, and **Lyrian**, a high-elf bard who was imprisoned there. **Sir Edric** is with them. **Lulu** is safe with Elias and the civilians in the residential district (location 14).

The House is where the Ashen "remake" people. Prisoners drink prepared **River Styx water** in the **chamber of the waters**, and it erases who they are. Edric's knights drank, and **the Mountain** now speaks ("Born anew") but is "not himself anymore." The party escaped their cells and recovered their gear from the armory, but not the **bag of holding** or Brad's shield. Both are upstairs, where **Registrar Sen** keeps the key in her office. **The session ended at the start of a fight in the chamber of the waters.** The turned knights and two guards are hypnotized or asleep, and the Mountain, who resisted, has been blasted into the fountain.

Outside, **Ikaia** and **Lady Cerys** hold the Northern Bridge. Ikaia's bargain stands: strip the sunlight barriers from **Lord Ottmar's** residence for a wish, and remove "anybody that opposes" him (which includes the resistance) for his help once. He can be called on a **sapphire sending stone**.

## The party
- **Freyr Meliamne** (Jeff): elf Archfey warlock 5 / sorcerer 2, about 160 years old. His shapeshifting quasit familiar **Twitch** scouts invisibly. He opposed killing the cell guard but still took corruption for it (he was at 0 after 11/13). He wants to reach **the tree** for his patron and plans to take Devil's Sight next level.
- **Brad / Broderick Reinhardt** (Mouhssine): dragonborn Oathbreaker paladin. His ancestors were **Hellriders**. He leads Edric, who now says he has "lost faith" in him. His shield is upstairs. He has the Sending Stone, Prayer of Healing, Lay on Hands, and a Revivify scroll.
- **Silas** (Spencer): Moon druid, back after being taken on 11/11. He was sent to hell as a child about 500 years ago, fought the Hellriders, and has hunted bounties for **Zariel** for about a century. His current job is to **kill Ikaia**. His soul is bound to someone, and he wants to know who sold it and who bought it. He has memories of dying many times.
- **Lyrian** (Darrel, new 11/18): high-elf Glamour bard and traveling performer. He refused to serve the Ashen and was beaten for two weeks. The Ashen took his viol.
- **Tink** (Darrel): **hanged on 11/18**. Tink is now a "full-blown devil." **Apple** (Spencer) fell to Senezhael on 11/13. **Rhys** (Avi) is inactive and ran off with the resistance.
