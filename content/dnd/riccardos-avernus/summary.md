# Riccardo's Avernus: the story so far

## Where things stand
The party is **level 7** and inside the **Keep of the Knights** with their 20 refugees. The keep holds **62 people** (45 civilians, 12 knights, 4 squires and the healer Brenna), commanded by **Sir Edric Vale**. The knights survived by making a **deal with Ikaia**: help no one outside, and keep the **Lantern of the Dawn** inside the walls, in exchange for food crates every fourth night. Letting the party in broke that deal. Edric asked Brad to sacrifice himself or some of the refugees, and Brad refused. Tink then convinced Edric to stand and fight. Tonight brings the last supply drop, and **Ikaia comes alone to the middle of the bridge**. Edric will meet him there with the party. Lathander has told Brad that only he can unlock the lantern, and that he must leave *the unworthy* behind.

- **Seraphina**: Freyr's patron. Her power is limited here, and she sees through his eyes.
- **Senezhael**: Apple's patron (CR 19), who appears as a girl. She holds **7** of the party's 20 refugee souls and can't pass the keep's pulse check. She sensed a power inside that even she can't see into.
- **Lord Ikaia**: vampire lord, CR 17, +12 to hit. His bite can turn a PC in one go. He prizes "fairness," and he has a deal with the keep, now broken.
- **Sir Edric Vale**: commander of the keep. Proud and harsh, he turns refugees away. He made the deal with Ikaia and keeps the lantern hidden in the temple altar.
- **The Mountain**: a silent seven-foot knight who is Edric's shadow.
- **Captain Alara Voss**: wounded captain who runs the Keep together with Edric and knows Greater Restoration. She defied Edric to answer the stone and is now out of spells.
- **Brenna**: the keep's generous healer.
- **Elias**: allied fighter.
- **Lulu**: holy elephant who calms the refugees.

- **Autumn**: refugee contact, gone to the Antlers / Amber Leaf.
- **The master**: butcher-apron vampire who fled the slaughterhouse.

## Sessions
- **The Slaughterhouse (2026-09-01):** The party raided a vampire slaughterhouse, staked the elder Marina, found the Vampiric Syphon scroll and rescued ten survivors.
- **The Northern Dock (2026-10-13):** The party took a lava barge to the Northern Dock in disguise. Tink's "Raphael" started a fight. The Star killed Freyr's familiar, and Brad's World draw broke his oath and turned the vampires human.
- **The Boulevard to the Keep (2026-10-17):** Brad's Lathander light ended the dock fight. Joven betrayed the party, and Senezhael audited souls. The Nightbringer broke a duel deal, and Ikaia killed him and Joven, revived the dead, and granted safe passage. The party reached level 7.
- **The Lantern of the Dawn (2026-10-23):** Brad was welcomed back into the keep. The party uncovered the knights' deal with Ikaia, and Brad refused Edric's sacrifice. Edric agreed to face Ikaia tonight. Lathander, through the lantern and Tink's tarot, named Brad its true bearer.
