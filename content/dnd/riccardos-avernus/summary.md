# Riccardo's Avernus: the story so far

## Where things stand
The party is **level 7** and trapped inside the **Keep of the Knights** with no food and no way out. The keep holds **62 people** (42 from before plus the party's 20 refugees), commanded by **Sir Edric Vale**. Edric's **deal with Ikaia** is broken. The knights now know the truth: they survived by hiding the Lantern of the Dawn, helping no one outside, and taking food drops every fourth night. Brad and Tink convinced them to have faith and stand together.

Freyr and Apple went to the supply drop and found **vampires instead of food**. They lied their way out and brought back crates of trash to keep the civilians calm. Apple prayed to Yondala, and **the Star** revealed two lights: temporary safety at **the Antlers** and ultimate safety—**a way home**—at **location 16**. The party has three choices: make a frail deal with Ikaia (Temperance), take boats with divine judgment (Justice), or march out the front gate with the lantern (the Emperor). No choice yet. The keep has food for one more meal. Edric doubled the guard and is pacing the battlements, waiting for Ikaia's response.
