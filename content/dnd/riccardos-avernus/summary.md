# Riccardo's Avernus: the story so far

## Where things stand
The party is **level 7**. The keep is lost and the Lantern of the Dawn is gone. They still owe **Ikaia** a cleared bridge to hand to **Lady Cerys**. From **the Antlers** (location 8, where **Emberleaf** is), they staged about 87 civilians at a scout post and struck the **Northern Bridge** in a disguised cart.

On 11/11 the mid-bridge fight ended in a win, but at a cost. A **crowned figure** with a Witch-king-like horned helmet (Brad recognized it) and blazing yellow eyes rose from under the bridge. He frightened everyone, accused **Silas** of betraying his patron, and vanished with him in green smoke. He left **angel feathers**, cream-white and striped with black. Without Silas or Apple, the party called in **the Mountain**. Together they killed the captain and broke the Ashen line. Freyr finished the **arcanist** with Booming Blade. As he died, a trapped soul screamed out of him. **The party won the Northern Bridge fight and killed every enemy**, including the captain and arcanist. The knights hold the **center of the bridge**, but a **checkpoint on the far side** still blocks the crossing. Next session starts there.
