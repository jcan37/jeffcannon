# Riccardo's Avernus: the story so far

## Where things stand
The party is **level 7** and down to three: **Freyr, Tink and Brad**, plus **Lulu**, who is hiding with the civilians. On 11/13 they took the **Northern Bridge** and handed it to **Ikaia**. Before that, **Apple** fell holding off **Senezhael** so the refugees could cross. The 87 civilians scattered into western Elturel through the **sewers** and are hiding in the **residential district** (location 14). **Elias** stayed with Autumn, and **the Mountain** and the knights went to rescue captured refugees.

Ikaia's new bargain: strip the sunlight barriers from **Lord Ottmar's** residence for a wish, and remove "anybody that opposes" him in exchange for his help once. He can be called on a **sapphire sending stone**. The resistance opposes Ikaia too.

Western Elturel ("New Elturel") looks peaceful, with an air of hope. Old gold is no longer accepted, and nobody but guards carries arms. Freyr was picked up at an alchemist's, and Tink and Brad at an inn. All three were taken to the devil-guarded **House of New Names** (location 11). Their breakout without armor went wrong. Tink was caught, drew the Hanged Man and made his stand, and Freyr and Brad turned back for him. **The session ended with Tink (yellow card) and Brad down and Freyr alone in his Fog Cloud.** Riccardo told everyone to think about new characters.
