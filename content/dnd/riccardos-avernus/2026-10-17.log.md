## Rulings summary
- **Lathander's dawn:** 15 minutes, plus 2 minutes per HP spent, tracked as counters. Giving all your HP keeps it up until everyone is safe.
- **Vampires:**
  - Staking only works on a downed vampire.
  - Bites hit temp HP before max HP.
  - A bite that drops you to 0 can turn you.
  - Undead souls can't be claimed.
- **Combat:**
  - Hits on held or paralyzed targets are automatic crits.
  - Help is an action.
  - A grappled creature has disadvantage attacking anyone but its grappler.
  - Firing blind into magical darkness is at disadvantage.
- **Scrolls:** using one out of combat needs an Arcana check.
- **Elias** makes death saves like a PC (average +1).
- **Brad's damage reduction:** his "half damage" is now a reaction against one physical attack, plus −3 to physical damage.
