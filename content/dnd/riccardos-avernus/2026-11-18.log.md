## Scene 1: Captured
- Riccardo struck a match at the table ("Someone is dying"). The horde of devil enforcers, mages and Ashen warriors overwhelmed them, and the Ashen government captured all three. They were **stripped of everything except what they wore**. No weapons, no shields, no boots and no bags. Riccardo told them not to erase the gear from their sheets, because there's a chance to get it back.
- They were marched in a column through fallen Elturel, past frightened people, with guards poking them along. At a stop someone said: *"Blindfold them. We're bringing them to the house."* After 20 to 30 minutes of walking they were taken indoors.
- Tink rolled Survival with advantage (cartography) for 12 and lost track of the route. The cells are cold, "a bit of a paradox here in hell." They are tiny and "meant to make you very uncomfortable." Later it turns out they are **one level below ground**.
- Freyr and Brad were put in one cell, and Tink in another with a **silent tiefling** who sits balled up, tail wrapped around him, staring into the void. Riccardo played the Hitman theme ("the assassin's song").
- One thing was not taken: **their soul coins are still on them**. Freyr kept his **cloak of protection**.

## Scene 2: The other prisoners
- An **isolated cell**, whose only opening is its bars, holds **Silas** ("Silas Mitrush," uncertain), naked. Spencer is back as Silas.

The "Ashen government" tried to recruit him once they saw his magical potential. He refused, and they beat him every day to make him serve. He has been there **two weeks, "ever since the city fell."** Upstairs is "a throne or ritual room" where they make you **drink from a cup**, and after that you "come all that." He has always refused.

## Rulings summary
- Captured PCs keep only what they wear (no boots). Gear may be recovered.
- Anti-magic lining (bars, keyhole, rings): touching it ends illusions, wild shape and incantations.
- Long rest and short rest are exclusive before the morning. One ration from dinner counts toward long-rest benefits.
- Suggestion: up to 25 words, said in one go, and it ends early once the activity is complete. Command is one word and lasts one turn.
- Augury covers a course of action in the next 30 minutes and takes 10 minutes per ritual cast.
- Wild shape reverting in too small a space normally puts you in the nearest space that fits. Riccardo allowed it once. Wounds taken in beast form carry over.
- Corruption: killing Ashen Guard soldiers gives it, and killing devils does not. Enabling or threatening counts.
- Pass without Trace is a flat +10. Group stealth needs half the group to pass. Bardic inspiration stacks on top of guidance, and guidance doesn't apply to attacks.
- Silence is a visible bubble (like heat haze) and breaks Suggestion concentration. Sleep (2024) is not concentration and lasts a minute. Hypnotic Pattern incapacitates for a minute.
- The turned Mountain has advantage on saves against spells.
- Potions need hands. Alchemist's supplies proficiency gives advantage to identify a substance (Intelligence).
