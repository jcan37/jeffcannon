## Recap and setup
The session picks up mid-raid on a vampire **slaughterhouse** ("location four"). Outside last time, the party killed the vampire on patrol. Riccardo explained the setup:
- **Silvia** gave the party a set of **spell scrolls**. Riccardo kept back only *"the deck,"* so tarot draws now come through prayer.
- Brad is still **flying** (about 10 minutes left).
- The NPC **Lulu**, who has a "trumpet/flute" power once a day, is with them. They plan to use her daily power to track time in Avernus, where time feels unreliable.
- Riccardo showed a newly painted mini, **"Edgar."** The table joked about someone having a grudge against Edgar's "champion" *(context unclear)*.
- Character plans:
  - Freyr is multiclassing **warlock/sorcerer** and will go Wild Magic at sorcerer 3.
  - Apple plans 3 levels of **ranger** for Hunter's Mark and the archery style, to boost his dagger throws.
  - Tink is staying a straight fighter.

## Scene 1: Through the roof window
- Freyr's **quasit Closet** broke a closed second-floor window (Riccardo ruled that breaking it takes an action).
- **Rappelling ruling:** you must be in the square directly above. Going down **costs 20 ft of vertical movement**. An Acrobatics or Athletics check halves it. Jumping instead means falling damage.
- Tink tied his rope (he also has a grappling hook) to a **chimney** about 10 ft back with an object interaction.
- **Apple** went in first, using a bonus-action Dash to rappel. The room was a dark **office** with accounting tables and **two big metal chests**. Apple **locked the door with his lockpicks**; with no key, that took a main action. Ruling: opening a chest is a main action. It wasn't trapped.
- **Chest 1:** **4 Potions of Healing.** Apple drank one (2d4+2) and shared the rest out *(final holders roughly one each; uncertain)*. Then he hid in Lulu's shadow (halfling).
- Brad hovered above the inside of the door with a readied **smite** for anyone coming through. Freyr cast **Protection from Evil and Good** from one of Silvia's scrolls. The table discussed Freyr using the **potion of invisibility** before casting.
- Perception: two players heard muffled **screams from far below**, *"of somebody that's being cut alive."*
- Freyr used his **rod** to regain a spell slot.

## Scene 2: Chest 2, the ledger, and the scroll
- Banging on the door. Riccardo said several presences were outside. Tink **barricaded** with lumber from the bag of holding using his **carpenter's tools**. Ruling: that gives disadvantage on their attempts to break in.
- **Chest 2** was a much harder lock. Apple failed at 9, then got **20** with a bonus-action retry. Contents:
  - **old business ledgers**
  - a **pouch of corroded gold coins**, *"eaten half… more like hundreds of years"* old
  - **a scroll**
- **The scroll:** Freyr rolled **Arcana 20**. It's a **School of Necromancy, "Forbidden Knowledge"** spell, *"a variation of a spell invented by a vampire lord more than a thousand years ago."* This is the scroll the party knows as **Vampiric Syphon**.
  - 1 action, 60 ft, the target must be seen, **no attack roll**. It deals about **5–6 necrotic**.
  - Regaining HP: you can **spend 2 regained HP to restore 1 lost max HP**, and can go up to **8 over max**.
  - It can be **read but not learned**. Riccardo hinted there **may be corruption attached**. *"A necrotic fireball with some healing."*
- **The ledger:** normal entries for livestock and meat sales, then blank pages, then daily human tallies: **Received, Consumed, Converted, Remaining, Potential**.
  - Latest entry: **Received 34, Consumed 7, Converted 3, Remaining 24, Potential 5.** (Riccardo first said 37, then corrected himself to 34.)
  - "Converted" rarely rises. When "Potential" drops, "Converted" sometimes rises by 3 the same day.
  - **The latest entry is dated about a week ahead** of what the party thinks is today. Time in Avernus is off.
- They left the ledgers in the office.

## Scene 3: The pincer attack
- Plan: Brad, Freyr's quasit Closet and Lulu hold the office. **Apple and Tink rappel down** to hit the ground floor from behind. Freyr goes later.
- Apple made Acrobatics and landed with a backflip (5 ft of movement). He saw a lookout in a brightly lit area staring up at the balcony. He hid behind **crates and barrels** and later **under the stairs** (Skulker gives advantage). His Stealth was high all night: 18, 20, 23, 24, 28, 29.
- **Corruption check:** Riccardo asked for corruption scores. One player said **11** *(probably Tink)*. That player sensed *"something dark is happening here,"* with someone lurking in the alcove.
- **Apple's spoons:** he **threw spoons** to distract the guard at the main doors. It only distracted the minion next to him. *"One of these times you've got to let the spoon strategy work."*
- More screams, this time of **hunger**: *"a woman's scream… almost a scream of liberation."* **A captive had just turned.** Riccardo: spawn are weak to **running water** and **holy water**.
- **The human "familiar"**, the vampire's servant directing the peons, shouted **"Leave them for the master!"** and all the thugs ("tough guys") converged on him. **The main doors were chained shut**, and a thug was working to free the "master" inside.
- **Apple** got sneak attack on the thug at the chain.

## Scene 4: Hunger of Hadar and the master
- **Freyr** cast **Hunger of Hadar** at third level, the warlock's slot level (*"all his spells are always max level"*). It made a **20-ft sphere** covering the chained doors: blinded, difficult terrain, **2d6 cold at the start of a turn and 2d6 acid at the end**. It dropped his Protection from Evil concentration. Ruling: it isn't magical darkness, so darkvision sees into it.
- The thug broke the chain and was ground down by cold and acid.
- **Tink** made several attacks in one turn: Cleave, then a **polearm butt strike** ("straight to the balls") for 11, 11, 18 and 17. *"Every time the blade master goes, it's like a ten-minute turn of rolls."*
- A **swarm of bats** flew through the sphere and became **a man in a butcher's apron, the master**. Freyr's quasit Closet stung for 5 and **poisoned** him, and **Eldritch Blast** hit for **19**.
- The servant **opened his own neck to his master** to heal him. The master **turned into bats and fled** above, behind the balcony.
- Ruling: a person **only turns into a vampire if a vampire drains all their blood**. A higher form of vampire is needed to turn someone; spawn can't do it.

## Scene 5: Marina
- **Tink** peeked into the feeding room and **failed a Dex save (6)**. He was **pulled in and grappled** by a female vampire: *"Who are you to interrupt my meal?"*
- **Escape ruling:** a Str or Dex check, and breaking free deals **psychic damage based on corruption** (1d4 if under 8). Tink broke free on a 17 and took 3 psychic.
- She tried to recruit him: *"Do you want to join us… partake in the eternal hunt?"* and *"I'm into bringing people to their fullest potential."* Tink played along: *"The master loves me."*
- The **new spawn** (a woman in blood-soaked rags, just turned and fed) charged out. Riccardo later sent the group a picture of the elder vampire and named her **Marina**. *(A survivor's account was transcribed as "Vienna"; most likely the same person.)*
- **Necrotic and max-HP ruling:** physical damage applies first, then necrotic. **Necrotic reduces max HP** whether or not you have temp HP. Halving damage halves the max-HP loss too, rounding down.
- **Crit ruling (new):** on a natural 20 you may **give up the double damage to inflict a condition** instead. Freyr used this with **Eldritch Blast** so the push broke Marina's grapple on Tink.
- Freyr used **Mind Sliver** (−1d4 on the next save) and Closet's **Scare** (frightened). He later dropped Hunger of Hadar for **Thorn Whip**, which yanked a vampire 10 ft up so it fell prone. Brad used his **Protection** reaction to cover allies.
- Tink drew **Hellreaver** (*"not awakened, but it's here"*) and hit her for 14.

## Scene 6: Apple prays, and the tarot answers
- Five more enemies were seen coming down the alley, **back from a raid, led by a paler figure**.
- **Apple** spent his whole turn **praying to Yondala**, his halfling mother's goddess, *"for protection… of me, my allies, and all of the living in this building."*
- Riccardo offered a **tarot draw** or a guaranteed half-strength effect. Apple drew a tarot: **The Tower (16).**
  - Earthquake and lightning. Everyone made Dex saves or fell prone. The vampire fell.
  - It **held the raiding party back two turns**.
  - The Tower can't come up again this session.
  - Riccardo: *"You have the Eye of Yondala on you."*
- When the earthquake ended, **one player alone heard a whisper**, most likely **Brad** *(Mouhssine answered it; uncertain)*: **"You didn't even try to save them. You're so bloodthirsty."**
- Marina bit Apple and drained max HP. Apple's second prayer went to **"Dala Don"** *(as heard)*, **Yondala's darker alter ego**, *"who would accept darkness to save the good,"* a secret his father, who taught him to steal, had shared. He drew **The Empress**.
  - Bickering voices, then a soothing one: *"I'm going to prove I am the better one, brother."*
  - **Everyone who lost max HP regained half of it**, along with that much current HP.
- Riccardo listed the gods who can grant tarot boons:
  - **Yondala and her alter ego** for Apple
  - "**Leander**" (also heard "Nathander") and **Seraphina** for others *(who belongs to whom is uncertain)*
  - **Raphael**, who grants boons but not tarots
  - **patrons**, whose favors must be paid back
  - **Not "Zel"**: *"No one can ask for help from a devil."*

## Scene 7: Marina falls
- Apple broke a later grapple with an acrobatic kick (18). *"For the first time in my life, the gods are on my side,"* and he stabbed.
- **Brad** had attacked once all night. He went in with **flail and smite**:
  - First: 12 necrotic + 9 bludgeoning, then a doubled radiant smite. She was **bloodied**.
  - Next turn: **16 necrotic + slashing**. **Marina dropped**, and Brad **staked her**. *"Holy shit! That's never worked before."*
  - Stake ruling: always 1d6 piercing. It destroys her only if she's incapacitated or brought to 0.
- The raid leader saw it and screamed **"Marina!"** He was her husband or partner. Then he **retreated**, and every presence faded.
- Freyr **Misty Stepped** down and Closet closed one of the doors.

## Scene 8: The larder and the survivors
- In the pens: **piles of bodies drowned in blood**. It was a human "harvesting place," where people were held like cattle and fed to the vampires and their servants. **Ten survivors**, including **two children**. They described one captive turning and eating others alive *"under the influence of a woman vampire."*
- There was also a survivor or relative called **"Mike"** whose wife was mentioned *(garbled; uncertain)*.
- **Basement:** a 15-ft ladder down to one big room of drained bodies with bite scars. **The party staked them all.** One **gasped awake and turned to ash** when staked. They staked the bite-marked bodies upstairs too, after taking the children out.
- **Loot from the enemies:** plain daggers and clubs. The vampires left only ash. **Vampire ash** was bottled in an empty oil bottle *(probably Freyr's)*. Riccardo ruled **no alchemy system**: *"If we play with alchemy, all your spells need materials."*
- The survivors used to live across the city before the bridges were destroyed and the lockdowns began. They had been at **the refuge, the hospital**, as patients and helpers. They know **Autumn** (*"still safe"*).
  - **The hospital is no longer safe.** Only **"the Antlers"** and **the fort up north** remain as safe houses.
  - The quickest route north is **the docks**, where they've heard of a **boat that sails the molten lava**.
  - One survivor, a former runner, knows of an **abandoned warehouse between 4 and 6C** that might be safe.
- Brad wanted to paint the **resistance leaf** on the door. The survivors stopped him: *"If you paint that leaf, people will think that this is a safe place."*
- **Rations:** **2 to the children**, and **10 deducted** from the bag of food. Ruling: **each refugee eats 1 ration per long rest**.
- **Corruption:** Riccardo said getting these people to safety will cut corruption by *"a fuckton."* **Apple got −1 corruption now** for paying for their safety.
- The party moved the survivors up to the clean **office** and took a **short rest**. They decided against a long rest here. Session ended.

## Rulings
- Rappelling costs 20 ft of vertical movement; Acrobatics or Athletics halves it.
- Breaking a window takes an action. Opening a chest is a main action.
- 2024 hiding rules: invisible to creatures you've beaten on Stealth; a new creature needs a new check.
- Throwing range is in squares equal to your Strength score *(as stated)*.
- Turning requires full exsanguination by a higher vampire.
- Necrotic reduces max HP; physical damage applies first.
- Grapple escape deals psychic damage based on corruption.
- Crit: you can trade double damage for a condition.
- Prayer draws a tarot. A card drawn is out for the session.
- No alchemy. Refugees eat 1 ration per long rest.
