## Recap

At the Elturel slaughterhouse (location 4), the party went in from the roof through the second-floor office window. Tink tied a rope to a chimney, and Freyr rappelled in and locked the padlock from inside. The office looked out over the slaughter floor.

Freyr cast Hunger of Hadar on the floor. A sentry unchained the feeding-room doors for "the master," a vampire in a butcher's apron who came in as bats, took damage, and fled upward. He is still at large. Marina, the vampire in the feeding room, was dropped by Brad and staked. Her partner fled.

Apple prayed to Yondala and drew The Tower: an earthquake and lightning knocked enemies prone and delayed a returning raid party by two turns. Apple then prayed to Dala Don and drew The Empress, which restored half of everyone's lost max HP.

In the larder the party found 10 living captives, including 2 children, and a pit of blood-drained corpses below. The survivors were moved toward the upstairs office. The party took a short rest; a long rest wasn't safe.
