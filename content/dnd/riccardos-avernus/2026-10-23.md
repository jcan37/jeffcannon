## Recap

The keep stands on a bridge over the lava, under a sign: *"The night has not won. Approach in the light."* Brad said he had trained as a knight of Lathander before the fall. He couldn't remember the oath, but he remembered a training technique, and **Sir Edric Vale** said, *"Welcome home, brother."* Senezhael couldn't get past the pulse checks at the gate. Before she faded, she said something inside keeps the vampires out, something even she can't see into.

Inside are a dozen knights, the wounded **Captain Alara Voss**, the healer **Brenna**, and a silent seven-foot knight called **the Mountain**. Brenna gave the party a free short rest and gave Freyr potions. Tink learned that Alara had wanted to answer the sending stone and Edric forbade it. She went anyway, lost a knight, and spent her last spells healing the bitten. She also let slip the order's secret: the **Lantern of the Dawn**, a light vampires can't cross.

Edric took Brad aside and made an offer. The knights have a **deal with Ikaia**: they let no one in, help no one outside, and keep the lantern inside the keep, and in return they get food crates at the dock every fourth night. Letting the party in broke that deal, and Edric's way to make it right was to sacrifice Brad or some of the refugees. Brad refused (*"I cannot sacrifice one to save ten"*) and told the party. Tink's speech won Edric over. Ikaia is expected tonight at the last supply drop, alone, in the middle of the bridge. Edric will go out to meet him, and the party will stand with him.

In a locked temple, Edric took the lantern out of a hidden compartment in the altar. Every knight knelt and looked away from it. Its power grows in the hands of a true knight, one who hasn't broken his oath. Tink prayed to Lathander on Brad's behalf and drew The High Priestess, The Hanged Man and The Sun. Brad learned that **only he can unlock the lantern's full potential**, and that to do it he must leave behind *the unworthy*: the ones who turn their eyes from its light. Freyr quietly took out the ceremonial dagger. It's magical, but he'll have to attune to it before it gives up its secrets. Then the table went to the bar.
