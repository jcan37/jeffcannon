## Recap

Brad prayed to Lathander, and dawn rose from the Companion. He became a 20-ft bubble of light that vampires and thralls can't enter without burning. It runs on a timer that he can feed with his own HP. Later he used all his Lay on Hands on himself and poured 38 HP into the light, dropping to 1 HP. Brad and Apple hit the Nightbringer hard, and he fled as a bat.

Freyr broke the rune on the sending stone. A voice on the other end said the boulevard is the fastest way to the keep, but the streets are full of vampires. Two natural 20s got Freyr into the quartermaster's office and chest. Apple freed the refugees and set the barge on fire, though the vampires put it out. Joven's wife wasn't at the dock. When the party wouldn't go back for her, he ran to the vampires, and the Nightbringer drained him.

On the march, Senezhael (Apple's patron) appeared as a child and asked to audit the refugees' souls. Captain Malakorum, the champion of Edgar, challenged Fry to a duel. Brad held him in place, Freyr hit him for 30, and the shadows took him. Apple then made a deal: Senezhael could identify her souls but not claim them. She marked six or seven as hers. Arrows and panic cost about a dozen refugees before the gate.

The Nightbringer had healed by feeding on Joven, and he blocked the gate while Lord Ikaia watched from a rooftop. Apple arranged a 5-on-5 fight. Before it started he drew The Empress, which gave the party a full heal, a long rest and 1d20 temp HP each. Senezhael stole the Nightbringer's identity and tore into him. Losing, he begged Ikaia for help and broke the deal with a Nightfall that dropped the party and killed 12 civilians. Senezhael then killed him.

Ikaia stopped the fight. He finished the Nightbringer and cut the newly turned Joven to pieces. He crushed the Nightbringer's fangs into dust that revived the dead civilians, leaving 20, and gave the party safe passage to the keep with a warning never to return. Darrel was away, so Tink stayed in the background.
