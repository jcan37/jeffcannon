## Scene 1: The keep
- The keep is built on a bridge lined with houses (Riccardo compared it to Florence), with the lava river below. It has outer and inner bastion towers. The **right tower has collapsed** and is dark, and the **left is lit**.
- Under the keep is a small **private military dock** above the lava, with a melted gate. It's big enough for a canoe or life raft.
- Singed banners of **Lathander's rising sun**, and a sign: *"The night has not won. Approach in the light."*
- Sentries: *"Stop there. Lower your weapon. Speak your name before dawn, and tell us whether any of you have been bitten."*
- The party had **20 refugees**. Riccardo: four of them are Senezhael's. *(DM slip: Senezhael actually holds 7 souls, not 4.)* Senezhael walked with them as a girl of about ten.

## Scene 2: Welcome home, brother
- **Brad:** *"We're the ones who called upon you… I am the glowing light."* He swore nobody had been bitten (OOC: *"I got bitten a lot"*).
- Asked for the knights' oath, Brad couldn't recall it (History 8, then 8 again).
- He introduced himself as Brad of the **Reinhardt** family *(as heard)*, trained as a knight of Lathander in the city before it fell, and back from the Material Plane. His last mission was to retrieve a shield, and his family was slaughtered over it.
  - Riccardo: *"Is this the actual shield of the Hidden Lord?"* Brad: *"Does it look like the shield of the Hidden Lord to you?"* (Also heard as "the Garog shield." *Uncertain.*)
- A third History roll (about 10–11) brought back a specific training technique. **Sir Edric Vale**, Knight of Lathander: *"Welcome home, brother."*
- **Captain Alara Voss**, wounded and on a crutch, coordinated the refugees. Crossbows stayed trained on the party while knights **checked everyone's pulse** at the gate.

## Scene 3: Senezhael at the threshold
- Senezhael: *"That means I can't get in."* She talked bargains and threatened to claim her souls on the spot.
- Brad argued that the vampires can't take the keep. Her answer: *"There is something very powerful that's preventing them, that even I can't peer into. You may actually want to ask him that… Remember, ask him."* Then she faded.
- Riccardo later clarified: she couldn't get in because of the pulse test, and the something inside is, *"to your knowledge, the lantern."*

## Scene 4: Inside the walls
- **Courtyard:** training grounds, a sandpit, kids playing a rock-tossing game, and the **Temple of Lathander**.
- **Who's there:** about 10–12 knights, 3–4 squires, the healer **Brenna**, and **the Mountain**, a seven-foot knight in full plate with his helmet down (race unknown). People move out of his way. He follows Edric like a shadow and never speaks.
- Knights keep to one side and civilians to the other. The walls are about 35–40 ft high.
- **Freyr's pets:** the knights balked at the quasit (*"looks like Stitch"*) and the zombie. Freyr's Persuasion, with an extra d6, was a **natural 20**. Both were let in, but the zombie has to stay in a corner. Freyr has no spell slots left to resummon it if it drops.
- **Edric** thought the keep was the last holdout. *"Not even the Ashen Government comes this far,"* because they're so deep in vampire territory. The tower fell *"during the fall."*
- **Tink** told him they'd come north looking for the Emberleaf (*"You mean the resistance?"*), got cut off in the marketplace, and took a boat. He showed his journal, his map and the vial of vampire ash.
- **Group Insight:** Tink got 23, helped by Freyr's Guidance (+d4) and Tactical Mind. Edric got very uneasy whenever Ikaia came up.
- **Brad and Edric clashed.** *"We just kicked the shit out of vampires in front of your doors… Have you forgotten your oath?"* Edric: *"If you were not a coward you would have been with us during the fall… I am the only one that keeps us safe."* Edric went off to confer with the Mountain.

## Scene 5: The healer
- Alara pointed the party to the apothecary. Brenna gave them a **free short rest**: 2 free hit dice, more can be spent, and no time passes. Tink got back to full.
- **Tink** asked who could cast **Greater Restoration**. He rolled Persuasion 11, plus Commanding Presence for 17, then Tactical Mind to reach the 20 he needed.
  - Brenna: only **Captain Alara** knows it.
  - When the sending stone call came through, Alara wanted to go. Edric refused, and she went anyway with **four knights**. They didn't make it a block, lost one, and came back badly hurt.
  - Edric has turned away people pleading at the gates. *"Brenda's mouth is gonna kill her someday."* The party are the first people let in in a long time.
- **Brad** asked Brenna what happened. The temple fell, and she'd been stationed here. Edric and his knights go down to the dock and come back with food. The Resistance only rose *after* the vampires took over.
- Brad prayed in the temple.

## Scene 6: Alara and the secret
- **Tink and Alara.** She was ambushed just off the bridge: *"as many eyes as there are stars in the night."* She'd spent her last slots on Greater Restoration for the bitten knights and needs time to recover.
  - Tink: *"I'm gonna level with you, lady. I've never been a part of any chain of command. I just fucking fight."*
  - *"You guys definitely have weapons here worth protecting."* Alara: *"We don't have weapons. We have something even better… Your brother should know."*
  - Persuasion with Commanding Presence: **25**. That revealed the **Lantern of the Dawn**, an artifact of the order whose light vampires can't breach. Alara had asked Edric for it so she could reach the docks, and he refused. *"The chain of command takes precedence."*
- **The axe:** Tink asked about weapons for Brad. The armory is reserved for knights, so he'd have to ask Edric.

A kid: *"We just need the magic sword."* The old man says it's only a tale to give them hope. Riccardo (OOC): it's story information, and the party may find something like it.

## Scene 8: Edric's offer
- Edric to Brad: *"Are you ready to do whatever is necessary to protect the people of Elturel?… You shall not speak of this with your companions."* Riccardo texted the offer to Mouhssine.
- **The offer, as Brad later shared it:** the knights have a **deal with Ikaia** and weren't supposed to let anyone in. To be forgiven, they'd have to **sacrifice Brad, or some of the souls he brought.**
- **Armory haggling:** Tink offered the quartermaster's plate armor. The armory countered with a plain battleaxe (*"that's a rip off"*). The knights use longswords, greatswords and heavy crossbows. Brad prefers his flail (Topple). **No trade was settled on tape.**

## Scene 9: Council
- Brad told the party about the offer. The deal is: nobody gets in, the knights stay inside, the lantern stays inside, and Ikaia supplies food and medicine.
- **Options:** take the lantern and run for the other side of the city, or fight Ikaia. Tink was keen to fight. Riccardo: Ikaia is **+12 to hit**, and *"can one-shot you and turn you into a vamp… with one bite."*
- Edric: *"Are you with us or against us?"* **Brad:** *"Every life is worth everything. I cannot sacrifice one to save ten."* Persuasion **25**.
- **Edric's confession:** *"I broke a contract with Ikaia… Everybody in here is dead."*
- **Headcount:** 45 civilians (including the party's 20), 12 knights, 4 squires and Brenna, for **62** in all, plus the party. Apple counts too.
- **The lantern:**
  - Edric and the Mountain carried it out of the temple when they escaped.
  - Its light reaches **10 ft from the bearer**. That's about 8 people truly safe and 12 more maybe.
  - Vampires *can* enter its light, but it hurts them, so they could grab someone and drag them out. It can't be enhanced with magic.
- **How the deal happened:** after two weeks of starvation, Ikaia came and asked for the commander. **Terms:** aid no one outside, and the lantern never leaves the keep. In return the knights get **food crates every fourth night** at the dock below, and peace.
- **Tink's Insight** (15) on what he knows of eastern Elturel: three factions.
  - **The Resistance:** civilians who refused to bow.
  - **Ikaia.**
  - **The Ashen Government:** organized and aided by devils, and a much bigger problem.
  - The east side is pure vampire territory, with no Ashen patrols. The knights are a fly to Ikaia, and his real threat may be the government.
- **Tink's speech:** *"Nothing is given. So fight with me. Ikaia has weaknesses."* *"Trust me, Baldur's Gate ain't that world."* Persuasion with advantage, 19, which took him to 25.
  - Edric: tonight's supply drop is the **last**. Ikaia will come *"when the shadows that are cast by the pillars of flame go deepest, in the middle of the bridge, alone. I will go out there. Will you stand with me?"* The party agreed. It's a few hours away.
- **Short rest prep:** Freyr cast **False Life** and **Mage Armor**. Tink is at **32 of 66** max HP and needs Greater Restoration. Jeff floated using the party's Greater Restoration scroll.

## Scene 10: The dock
- Edric showed them the dock. There are **two lava-proof rafts** (*"canoes or life rafts"*), each carrying 7–8, for **14–15 in total**. They have metal oars and are plated like the barge. The knights built one and found the other after the first delivery.
- The gates are melted at the waterline, so only the upper part over the lava is left.
- The canal runs one way toward the **Northern Dock**. The other way, it passes under the bridges and **falls into the void**. The bridges are the only way across. The knights tried this early on.
- Rafting west toward **9A** might work as a shortcut, but *"the channel is small. There's no really hiding."* Brad mentioned he has Fly.
- Tink wants the lava as a forge. He has blacksmith's tools but no anvil (*"like five hundred pounds"*).
- Ideas: go to the Resistance hideout (marked 6A on the players' map, as heard), fake a sacrifice (*"they want blood"*), or create a distraction.

## Scene 11: The lantern
- Tink told Edric that a devil, Senezhael, had felt the artifact and been scared off. Edric: *"She told us you are in bed with devils."* Tink: *"You made a deal with a vampire."* Edric: *"Fair point… We're just trying to survive."*
- After a round of rolls, Edric took them to the temple. Everyone else was sent out. Knights **shuttered the windows and locked the doors** so the light wouldn't show outside, leaving 6–7 knights inside, including Edric and the Mountain.
- Edric recited a prayer and moved the icon. A **secret compartment opened in the front of the altar**, and he lifted out the lantern, which gave off an extremely bright light. *"This is the lantern of Lathander, carrying the light of the dawn."*
- **Checks:**
  - Freyr's Arcana (with Guidance on himself): 13, a fail.
  - Brad's Perception: 18.
  - Tink's Insight: **23**. The moment the light came out, **every knight knelt and averted his gaze**, Edric included. The light can be looked at, so they're looking away by choice.
- **What they learned:** it's a legendary artifact. Its effect grows when it's carried by a true knight of Lathander, and Riccardo's hint was that a true knight is *"not a knight that broke their oath."*
- **Tink's prayer to Lathander, for Brad:** *"I'm not a good guy… But Brad tries. He tries… He's a good guy… We're gonna do our best to save everyone in this keep… I'm asking for your help, not for me… Do it for him."* Riccardo made him choose between the people, the temple and Brad, and he chose **Brad**.
- **Tarot** (Tink drew): **The High Priestess** (intuition, hidden knowledge found out), **The Hanged Man** (suspension, sacrifice, surrendering yourself for a new perspective) and **The Sun** (clarity, vitality, success against all odds, revelation of true purpose). (Someone also said "the world" as the cards came out. *Unclear.*)
- **The revelation:** only Brad can carry the lantern to its full potential. Every knight in the room has displeased Lathander. To fulfil his oath and purity, Brad will have to **leave the unworthy behind**. Brad: *"I feel like I'm gonna leave Eddy behind."*
- **Brad's three questions:**
  1. *How do I unlock its full potential?* *"Hold it with a pure heart and pure intentions."*
  2. *Who are the unworthy?* *"You will see them when they make themselves manifest… When you hold the lantern, and they fall outside its light."* The unworthy turn their gaze from the true light of the sun.
  3. *How long can it keep the vampires away?* *"As long as you can hold it. But should you wish to make it brighter… there's only a limit to what I can do, and it may exact a toll."*

- He didn't cast anything with the knights there. Arcana 22: it's magical. It requires attunement and deals double damage against undead.
- **Description:** a fine dagger in a steel sheath lined with black leather, with gold inserts at the tip and the throat. Probably extremely expensive.
- Freyr put it back in his bag. Around 11 pm the table moved to a bar to keep going in roleplay. **The tape ends there.**
