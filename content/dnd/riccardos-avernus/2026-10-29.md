## Recap

The deal with Ikaia is broken. Brad convinced Edric to gather the knights and tell them the truth—that the keep survived by hiding the lantern and aiding no one outside. Edric hesitated, then confessed to the bargain, but tried to frame Brad's coming as a "noble sacrifice." Tink called him out, and the knights wavered. Brad prayed, and the lantern's light grew brighter in his hands. Edric backed him. The crowd calmed, barely.

Freyr and Tink (with Apple quiet in the background) took two knights down to the supply drop. The room was empty. Vampires dropped from the ceiling. Freyr and Tink lied their way out—claimed a deal with Ikaia, offered the barrel people as a treat, and sent the vampires south to a false stash. They brought back crates of trash and clothes to keep the civilians calm, but Edric saw through it. The keep has food for one more meal, then nothing.

Apple prayed to Yondala for guidance. The Star came up. Two lights appeared across the city: a dim reflection from **the Antlers** (temporary safety) and a brilliant glow from the far side at **location 16** (ultimate safety, a way home). Above them, the sky was darker than ever—no hope from Ikaia. The party now has three choices, shown by the tarot: make a deal with Ikaia (Temperance), sneak everyone out by boat (Justice), or march out the front gate with the lantern (the Emperor). They're leaning toward the Emperor.

The keep is on edge. Edric doubled the guard. Captain Alara asked if they're all going to die. Freyr talked to a rat and stole booze. The Mountain fist-bumped Tink. Time is running out.
