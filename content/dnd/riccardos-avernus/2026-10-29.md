## Recap

The deal with Ikaia is broken. Brad convinced Edric to gather the knights and tell them the truth—that the keep survived by hiding the lantern and aiding no one outside. Brad told the knights the truth and asked Edric to speak. Edric hesitated, then admitted the deal while spinning it as Brad's "noble sacrifice." Tink called him out. Brad's second speech brought the crowd's stress down.

Freyr and Apple took two knights down to the supply drop. The room was empty. Vampires dropped from the ceiling. Freyr bluffed the vampires (Deception 22), claiming a separate deal with Ikaia and offering them barrels of the already-dead. He sent them south to a false stash. Back at the keep dock, Edric and his knights were waiting. Freyr and Apple talked them into carrying the trash crates up as if they were food (Deception 21 with Apple's help) until Freyr told Edric the truth. The keep has food for one more meal, then nothing.

Apple prayed to Yondala for guidance. The Star came up. Two lights appeared across the city: a dim reflection from **the Antlers** (temporary safety) and a brilliant glow from the far side at **location 16** (ultimate safety, a way home). Above them, the sky was darker than normal. Brad prayed to Lathander and drew The Wheel of Fortune, The Magician, and The Hermit; he chose The Magician. Freyr prayed to Seraphina and drew Temperance, Justice and the Emperor. The party now has three choices: make a deal with Ikaia (Temperance), sneak everyone out by boat (Justice), or march out the front gate with the lantern (the Emperor). No choice yet.

The keep is on edge. Edric doubled the guard. Captain Alara asked if they're all going to die. Freyr talked to a rat and stole booze. The Mountain fist-bumped Apple. Time is running out.
