## Recap

The fight outside the House of New Names ended the only way it could. The Ashen took **Freyr, Brad and Tink** alive and stripped them of everything but their clothes and armor. Their **soul coins** were the one thing left on them. Then they were marched blindfolded to "the house" and thrown into cells one floor below ground. Their cellmates were a silent, broken tiefling; a beaten high elf in rags; and, in a separate cell lined with anti-magic metal, **Silas**, alive and naked.

Guards took Freyr and Brad up to a courtyard with a dry fountain of **River Styx water**. Tink stood on a gallows with a noose around his neck. A herald praised **Zariel**, "our Queen and Eternal." Tink's last words were for her: *"The only sin is what I'm gonna do to Zariel once I find her."* The trapdoor dropped, and **Tink is dead**. Riccardo says **Raphael** has claimed Tink's sword, and Tink may come back as a full devil.

Back in the cells, **Sir Edric** turned up stripped and beaten. His surviving knights drank the waters and "turned." **The Mountain** can speak for the first time in 35 years, and he is "not himself anymore." Drinking the prepared Styx water wipes a person's identity. Everyone is due for their "rebirth" in the morning.

The high elf is **Lyrian** (Darrel's new character), a traveling performer and Glamour bard. He refused to serve the Ashen and has been beaten daily for two weeks. **Silas** says he was raised in hell from about age five, is roughly 500 years old, and fought against the Hellriders. He has hunted bounties for Zariel for about a hundred years. His current job is to **kill Ikaia**, and he waited on the bridge for strong fools to help. Somebody sold his soul, and he has spent centuries trying to learn who bought it. Brad and Freyr don't trust him.

They short rested. Lyrian's song gave everyone heroic inspiration, and Freyr's familiar **Twitch** scouted the floor above. Lyrian's **Suggestion** made the cell guard lock himself in a cell and hand over the key. Silas crawled into the guard's ear as a spider, and the guard told them that **Registrar Sen** keeps the key to the upstairs door. When the guard stopped being useful, Silas changed back to his own form inside the man's head. Riccardo handed out corruption.

Under Silence, Brad's frost breath and a hard shove broke the iron armory door. They got back their weapons, armor and backpacks, plus healing potions. Missing: the **bag of holding** and Brad's shield, which Locate Object puts upstairs. Freyr picked a lock with a natural 20, and the crates gave up more potions and one unknown potion.

They sneaked onto the balcony above the **chamber of the waters**. Below, the knights who drank kneel at a fountain. Lyrian, disguised as the dead guard, bluffed two guards. Then his **Hypnotic Pattern** and Freyr's **Sleep** put them down. Edric's stealth failed, and the kneeling knights stared him into terror. "Born anew," said the Mountain. A second Hypnotic Pattern locked up everyone below except the Mountain, who also shrugged off Brad's Hold Person. Silas missed, and Freyr's Eldritch Blast knocked the Mountain into the fountain. The session ended at the start of combat.
