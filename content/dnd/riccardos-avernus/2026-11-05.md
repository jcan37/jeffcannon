## Recap

At the Antlers, Nella walked the party through a marked map of eastern Elturel. Safe houses keep falling, the hospital is gone, and after the slaughterhouse and the docks the vampires are hunting hard. The Southern Bridge is already destroyed. The Middle Bridge is a fortress with mages on both ends. The Northern Bridge has cart barricades and magical sunlight lanterns that keep vampires off it — which is exactly why Ikaia needs the living to clear it.

The captive courier who swore Autumn fell tried to slip out the back. Then **Autumn herself** walked in, bloodied and torn, and Elias got his daughter back. Under interrogation the captive claimed to be **"Dane Voss," loyal servant of Lord Regent Ottmar Vale**, sole ruler of Elturel, and denied the resistance. Autumn named him **Corin Lyle**, one of her hospital scouts. He passed her dagger-notch memory test without drawing the blade, but his loyalty is Ashen and he hates the resistance. Zone of Truth and Detect Magic found no active charm or possession. His blood on the infernal tablet formed a **chain** — the same soul-bound mark the Hellriders at the market showed. Autumn and Elias refused to kill him; he stays bound and gagged.

The party left about **87 civilians**, half the knights, the squires, the apothecary, Elias and Autumn at a scout post two blocks from the bridge with one Sending Stone. Freyr, Tink, Brad and the knights took the cart: knights without Lathander tabards, loosely bound as "prisoners," Ashen badges and blood vials for cover. **Spencer's new character**, a Circle of the Moon druid already inside Ashen colors on the bridge (**Silas**), was waiting ahead. Apple stayed with the rear guard.

Freyr's Deception **29** sold the outer checkpoint. At the mid-bridge command pavilion the ruse collapsed when Silas haggled over buying the "pet" dragonborn and the tiefling for Soul Coins and the cart guards demanded a cut. Combat started. Freyr **Counterspelled** a Glyph of Confinement, then the mage **Hold Person**'d Brad. Silas Wild Shaped into a Huge giant constrictor snake and cast Conjure Animals (spirit serpents). Freyr opened Innate Sorcery and hit an Ashen soldier with an acid **Chromatic Orb** for 23. The fight for the Northern Bridge was still going when the session ended.
