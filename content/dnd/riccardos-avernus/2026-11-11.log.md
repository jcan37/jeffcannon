## Rulings summary
- The crowned figure's presence: frightened unless you roll a natural 20.
- Second Wind (2024) lets you move half speed without provoking. An action can be used for anything a bonus action could do, but not the other way around.
- Pole vault: Dash, 3 squares of runway, Acrobatics/Athletics plus half a superiority die. Below 10 is a critical failure. The halberd-snapping call was reversed.
- Grappling a creature halves your movement as you drag it. Paralyzed creatures automatically fail Strength saves.
- Hold Person ends only by breaking concentration, Dispel Magic, or a save-boosting ability, not by Help. The save is Wisdom.
- Brad's aura doesn't work while he's incapacitated. It adds +4 (his Charisma) to allies' saves, but not to skill checks such as Athletics.
- Mage recharge: big spells recharge on a die roll, easier for stronger mages. Senezhael's Finger of Death recharges on 5+.
- Calling an ally: the Mountain or Elias. The Mountain acts on Brad's initiative, and an adjacent PC directs him.
- The captain's aura gives advantage on each Ashen soldier's first save per turn within 60 ft. Lock Shields gives AC, and adjacent allies give +1 AC.
- "Yellow card": Riccardo's homebrew that replaces death saves, like soccer: you get a yellow card the first round down and a red card the second. Massive damage of double max HP (plus one) in one hit gives an immediate yellow card (exact rule uncertain).
- No cover system. A reasonable obstruction gives disadvantage.
- Reaction timing: disadvantage reactions before the roll; Shield after the hit; Counterspell after declaration; Interpose after a hit.
- Creatures larger than tiny (allies included) are difficult terrain to move through. Damage halves round down.
- Tendril spell: not concentration. Restrained until the end of the mage's next turn, then half damage again. Break free with an action and an Athletics check (DC 17).
- A spell targeting a point the caster can see doesn't work if the caster can't see the targets (inside Hunger of Hadar).
- Throwing flasks: an item interaction plus an attack each. Dropping them on your own square costs your action unless you free a hand.
