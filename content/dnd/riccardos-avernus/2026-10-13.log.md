## Scene 1: The locked compartment
- **Apple** picked the lock on the heavy **iron door** (high roll, about 24–29).
- Inside: a **feeding/holding room**.
  - Wooden poles with **chains and shackles**, stained **pitch black up to about 4 ft** from soaking in blood.
  - A sticky floor of dried blood (Survival check), and a smell of coagulated blood.
  - Every window is **blacked out with a flammable black wax**. No loot and nothing written on the walls.
- **Apple scraped the wax into two small vials.** Each makes a **1×1 burning square for 3 turns**. Apple plans to use them as **fake explosives**.
- **Infernal iron:** Freyr and Brad recognized the barge's **outer plating** as **infernal iron**, an alloy forged in hell that only infernal iron or magically imbued gems can cut or work. The barge itself is wood under the plating. **Hellreaver** is infernal iron. Normal steel and the black steel dagger are not.
- There's no anchor. The barge was simply tied to the dock.

## Scene 2: The river, Joven, and a new tarot rule
- **Rations:** they had **50 in the bag of food**. With **27 refugees**, each eating 1 per long rest, **27 were deducted**.
- **Joven's story:**
  - He ran the **southern dock** (supply intake). The barge carries the "human slush" barrels north to the **middle dock** (a civilian ferry station before the fall) and the **northern dock** (historically military, a block from the Knights' keep).
  - He cooperates only because the vampires hold his **wife and daughter at the northern dock**.
  - His sabotage: hiding maintenance keys, falsifying manifests, **watering down shipments**, and once smuggling someone out in an **empty casket**.
  - The **middle dock** is run by a human in the same position. The **northern dock** is run by a **vampire**.
  - The **Keep of the Knights** has a **hidden supply dock** underneath, reached by a narrow defensive channel. **The barge is too big for it.** Rumor says the Knights hold **relics that keep undead out**.
  - The **"Ashen government"** (devils) holds the area near the bridges. The further out you go, the more vampire territory it becomes.
  - Joven wants a **plan to free his family**, or to be dropped off. He mustn't be seen with the party.
- **The Antlers** belong to the refugees' faction. **The Amber Leaf** is their symbol, and **Autumn** went there.
- **New mechanic:** time in hell is wearing thin. **Each player may draw one tarot per session.** A roleplay draw lasts the whole session, but it replaces your combat draw. Each player heard part of a word from their patron, and the parts lined up when they compared notes.
- **Apple's patron**, heard as **"Senzel"** (also "Sanazelle"), teased him. Her favourite is *"a guy you will meet… in the garden under the shadow of a big oak tree."*

## Scene 3: The disguise and the middle dock
- **Setup:**

  - The barge normally carries a captain and four guards. A recruit made up the numbers: the **"16-year-old orphan" who had been suicidal**, to whom Apple gave daggers.

  - Ruling: getting down from the crow's nest takes a full turn of movement or a rope swing (Acrobatics). A 30-ft fall does 3d6.

## Scene 4: Planning the northern dock (6A)
- Long debate. The options were:
  - the raft
  - Lulu or a familiar flying a message to the keep (Lulu moves 120 ft a turn)
  - landing on the north shore
  - bluffing at the dock

- Ruling: **vampires sense living creatures and blood within 120 ft**, and pinpoint them better every 30 ft closer, **unless the creature is behind a fully sealed barrier**. The blood room was sealed, but scraping the wax broke the seal. Apple resealed it with a Dex check (with help).

  - a reaction that sends another vampire toward anyone who becomes bloodied

- **He can be talked to**, but he'll want a trade.

## Rulings
- A long rest restores drained max HP by rolling your hit dice.
- One tarot per player per session, as a bonus or main action. A roleplay draw replaces the combat draw.
- Vampires sense life within 120 ft unless it's behind a sealed barrier.
- Minor Illusion can't show a person.
- A 30-ft fall does 3d6. The Ring of Jumping needs a 10-ft run-up and doesn't reduce fall damage.
- Grappling needs a free hand. A shove is an unarmed attack contested by a Str save.
- Proficiency never adds to damage. A Cleave hit doesn't add your modifier.
- Recharge 5–6 abilities are checked at the start of each of the enemy's turns.
- Unstaked lower vampires come back in a few hours. Higher vampires fade back to their lair.
- Greater Restoration removes the max-HP penalty but doesn't heal.
